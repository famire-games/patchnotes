January 29 Update

A new character 'Cielle' has been added, along with the reworked 5v5v5 mode 'Crystal Clash', various map updates, and system changes. Character balance adjustments and weapon spec changes for Reyichi, Eika, Baimo, and others were also implemented.

Key changes

  • Added new Duelist character 'Cielle'
  • Introduced the 3-way 5v5v5 'Crystal Clash' mode replacing 7v7 Crystal Scramble
  • Temporarily applied Lunar New Year themed decorations to the Area 88 and Snowveil District maps
  • Revamped character skills and String Energy Networks for Reyichi, Eika, Baimo, Flavia, Audrey, and others
  • Optimized weapon spread increase logic during movement and aerial shooting
  • Hosted release-celebration event 'Moments With Cielle' for Cielle

Buffs

  • Ming primary weapon base damage increased from 27 to 28
  • Flavia primary weapon base damage increased from 21 to 22
  • Fragrance primary weapon base damage increased from 22 to 23
  • Increased reload speed for single-reload shotguns by 70%
  • Zephyr Blade heavy attack damage increased from 800 to 900
  • Wave Break heavy attack damage increased from 900 to 1150
  • Baimo's 'Freedom Rule' primary weapon reload speed increased by 35% and damage multiplier against Stringified targets increased to 75%
  • Fuchsia primary weapon gear base damage increased from 26 to 27 and active cooldown reduced
  • Secondary weapon Stinger base damage increased from 15 to 17

Nerfs

  • Zephyr Blade heavy attack wind-up time increased by 60%
  • Wave Break heavy attack wind-up time increased by 60%
  • Eika's damage bonus against Stringified targets at max heat state decreased from 25% to 20%
  • Fuchsia primary weapon base fire rate decreased from 650 to 630
  • Secondary weapon Ember spit firing delay increased from 0.2s to 0.28s
  • Secondary weapon Ignis projectile count increased and damage per projectile decreased from 9 to 8.5

Bug fixes

  • Increased ultimate energy point gain for Medi and Fragrance in Outbreak mode
  • Increased Map Workshop checkpoint delay settings and maximum limit to 15
  • Increased Map Workshop supply box maximum limit from 20 to 50
  • Adjusted Lilith's ultimate base damage in specific modes
  • Added audiovisual effects upon entering the final extraction point
