Hall of Art 'Seline's Cheeky Invitation' Open

Trickcal Re:Dive opens the second Hall of Art content, 'Seline's Cheeky Invitation'. Apostles can vote for their preferred theme theater to earn rewards and enjoy a rerun of the theme theater that takes 1st place.

Key changes

  • Hall of Art Season 2 content 'Seline's Cheeky Invitation' opens
  • Vote for the theme theater you want to see again
  • Claim rewards from the achievement screen after completing the vote
  • The theme theater selected as the final winner will be rerun on October 29
