Y11S3 Mid-Season Update Designer Notes

Covers balance changes for the Rainbow Six Siege Y11S3 mid-season update. It includes operator adjustments for Noor, Tachanka, Solid Snake, and Valkyrie, alongside changes to equipment and environmental elements.

Key changes

  • Buffed Noor's Horus Lance Launcher performance and response speed
  • Added an impact-detonation firing mode to Tachanka's Shumika Launcher
  • Reduced the precision mode charge amount for Solid Snake's Soliton Radar MK III
  • Added Glaive-12 to Valkyrie's weapon loadout slot
  • Increased fire damage and reduced Capitao's Fire Bolt deployment delay
  • Changed barbed wire to completely stop and self-detonate BU-GI

Buffs

  • Noor: Reduced Horus Lance Launcher drilling time, increased guard break intensity, reduced equip animation time, increased damage to 80, etc.
  • Tachanka: Added impact detonation firing mode for Shumika Launcher
  • Valkyrie: Added secondary weapon Glaive-12
  • Capitao: Reduced Fire Bolt deployment delay to 0.9 seconds
  • Clash: Increased enemy retreat speed reduction effect to 10%
  • Osa: Relaxed TALON-8 Clear Shield placement restrictions
  • Fire damage: Increased to 14 HP

Nerfs

  • Solid Snake: Reduced Soliton Radar MK III precision mode charges to 3
