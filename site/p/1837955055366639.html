Summer Extreme and Kazeros Raid Update

Kazeros Raid Act 1 Extreme difficulty and the Serca matching mode are added, and Solo Mode is expanded to Act 4 and Denuang. Additionally, Paradise Season 4, the Summer Night Festival event, and new summer avatars are released.

Key changes

  • Kazeros Raid Act 1 Extreme (Level 3 Difficulty) added
  • Solo Mode expansion (Kazeros Raid Act 4 and Denuang)
  • New Serca matching mode added (Lower difficulty and dynamic party scaling)
  • Paradise Season 4 begins
  • Summer Night Festival event held
  • New summer avatars released in the shop
