World of Warcraft Hotfixes (November 7, 2026)

These are the World of Warcraft hotfixes applied from late October to early November 2026. They include dungeon and raid difficulty adjustments, class balance patches, and bug fixes related to delves and labyrinths.

Key changes

  • Substantial reductions to key health and damage in Mythic and Heroic difficulties for the Undermine raid (Vaelkath, Stitched Vashj'ir, etc.)
  • Buffs to damage and healing for certain classes including Druid, Hunter, Mage, and Warlock
  • Stat nerfs for Rogue (Subtlety) and select PvP talents
  • Removal of Conquest/Honor caps and addition of bonus buffs in PvP content (Battlegrounds, Arenas, etc.)
  • Various progression-blocking bug and scenario error fixes for labyrinths and delves
  • Relaxed reputation requirements (Friendly) for Burning Crusade Classic Heroic dungeon keys

Buffs

  • Druid (Feral): All damage increased by 5% (PvP excluded)
  • Druid (Restoration): Verdant Infusion healing increased by 60%, Regrowth healing increased by 10% (PvP excluded)
  • Hunter (Marksmanship): Trick Shots ricochet damage increased to 85%, Explosive Shot and Multishot damage increased by 10%
  • Mage (Fire): All damage increased by 5% (PvP excluded)
  • Warlock (Affliction): Unstable Affliction damage increased by 20% (PvP excluded)
  • Warlock (Destruction): Rain of Fire damage increased by 20%

Nerfs

  • Rogue (Subtlety): Damage of all abilities decreased by 8%
  • Rogue (Trickster): Flummoxed now increases damage taken by 5% (down from 7%)
  • Death Knight (San'layn): Vampiric Strike damage decreased by 20% in PvP
  • Mage (Fire): All damage decreased by 5% in PvP

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where leaving a delve during the Basilisk Onslaught variant changed appearance and made allies hostile
  • Fixed tracking location guidance for Grilka the botanist in Nagrand
  • Fixed an issue where Great Vault progress was not granted in certain labyrinth scenarios
  • Fixed an issue where summoned entities or Sinister Eyes wrongly targeted non-combat enemies
  • Fixed an issue where item levels upgraded with Ascendant Venomstone were incorrectly applied
