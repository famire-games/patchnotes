Version 7.2 "Prelude to the End" Update

Genshin Impact Version 7.2 "Prelude to the End" has been updated, adding the new region 'Snezhnaya', new characters Mitya and Valerie, and new Archon Quests. In addition, third-person shooting mode content has been expanded, the Starlit Sea world system has been improved, and a number of bugs have been fixed.

Key changes

  • Opened the "New Kitezh City" and "Wing Ridge" areas in the new region "Snezhnaya"
  • Added new characters "Rule Breaker - Mitya (Electro)" (5-star) and "Thunder Fortress - Valerie (Electro)" (4-star)
  • Added new Archon Quest Chapter VII, Act V "Prelude to the End" and Act VI "Yesterday Sent from the Other Shore"
  • Updated third-person shooting mode combat content, new firearms, and stronghold system
  • Added Starlit Sea costume sets, lobby themes, and a like function
  • Updated new phases of Spiral Abyss and Imaginarium Theater

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where characters warped to abnormal locations in the Archon Quest "Requiem of Death"
  • Fixed combat withdrawal and abnormal behavior issues related to the enemy "Wandering Snow Guardian"
  • Fixed errors where some character models clipped and effects repeated continuously
  • Fixed Starlit Sea costume combination and mobile device Traveler model errors
  • Fixed cooking interaction button and voice unlock condition errors in the Snezhnaya region
