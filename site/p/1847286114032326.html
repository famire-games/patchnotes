Sea of Thieves Patch 3.9.1

The Sea of Thieves 3.9.1 update introduces custom seas feature expansion, costume storage, a toxic chat sanction system, and various bug fixes. In addition, various events such as Season 21 Community Weekend and Festival of the Damned are prepared.

Key changes

  • Added legendary locations such as 'Golden Sands Outpost' and new command/switch options to Custom Seas
  • Added 'Costume Storage' feature allowing players to save, rename, and delete costumes and vanity items (existing decorative chests removed and consolidated into costume chests)
  • Introduced a sanction (muting) system for players who maliciously abuse voice and text chat
  • Added mouse smoothing disable setting for mouse users
  • Various events and Twitch Drops including Season 21 Community Weekend, Festival of the Damned, and Reaper's Bones events
  • Added new items to the Pirate Emporium including Omen ship set, Raven pet variants, and Breath of the Dragon emote bundle

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where bailing water with a bucket and immediately sprinting does not cancel the bailing action and works properly
  • Changed it so players must remain nearby when reviving Skeleton Lord command skeletons
  • Shipwreck Graveyard door keys can now be obtained normally even after changing servers
  • Fixed an issue allowing players to sprint at the intended speed immediately after firing a Trident
  • Fixed an issue where the Reaper's Mark flag remained visible alongside alliance flags after diving
