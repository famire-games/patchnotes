Cities: Skylines II Free Update and Patch Overview

Cities: Skylines II is receiving a sizable free update featuring new building tools, leisure simulation overhauls, environmental enhancements, and various bug fixes. Players can now utilize new fences, surfaces, biomes, and improved public transport controls.

Key changes

  • Updated leisure simulation with visitor capacities, appeal values, and new notifications
  • New fence assets with road-snapping and custom colors, plus new surface materials like brick and concrete
  • Four new biomes (Alpine, Arid, Mediterranean, Tropical) with matching plants and props
  • Procedural terrain texturing along roads and advanced tree age customization
  • Public transport line vehicle counts can now be reduced to one with adjusted wait times
  • Parks & Recreation development tab unlocked earlier at Milestone 2

Bug fixes

  • Fixed citizens taking unnecessarily long detours to post mail
  • Resolved save thumbnails, autosaving, and crashes in large cities
  • Fixed citizens changing clothes halfway through a trip
  • Fixed pathways bending unexpectedly where they connect to roads
