11/10 Update Details

The November 10th update for Blue Archive features new swimsuit student pick-up and fest recruitment, an overhaul to the recruitment system, Main Story Part 2, and Korean voice acting. Additionally, a new event story 'Set Sail! The Chairwoman's Express' and Sinon's Battle Pass will be updated.

Key changes

  • New student pick-up recruitment for Makoto (Swimsuit), Satsuki (Swimsuit), etc.
  • Fest recruitment for Iroha (Swimsuit), Ibuki (Swimsuit), Rio, Neru (School Uniform) (3x ★3 rate)
  • Recruitment system overhaul (removal of standard recruitment, introduction of recruitment stacks and special stacks)
  • Main Story Part 1 Chapters 1 & 2 update
  • Korean voice acting added to Main Story Part 1 Final Chapter Chapters 1-4
  • Event story 'Set Sail! The Chairwoman's Express ~Makoto's Splendid Summer Vacation~' and mini-game

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where the additional Expert Permit acquisition effect was incorrectly displayed as +30% on the Mission (Hard) sweep screen
