Sea of Thieves Update 3.9.2

Sea of Thieves Patch 3.9.2 significantly reduces the overall installation size, expands Custom Sins, improves the free camera, and includes various gameplay quality-of-life improvements and bug fixes.

Key changes

  • Significantly reduced the game's overall installation size across all supported platforms
  • Added new legendary location 'Old Boot Fort' to Custom Sins
  • Enabled free camera entry into captain's quarters and below deck on all ship types and skeleton ships
  • Improved shop interface to view required commendations and progress simultaneously
  • Mitigated post-ranged weapon hit jump prevention and movement interpolation issues through network optimization
  • Various in-game events such as Season 21 Fame Rally and Last Ship Standing scheduled to be held

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where health bars were not displayed correctly when taking damage immediately after reaching full health
  • Resolved an issue where ship sails could not be raised or repaired after faction battles
  • Improved Megalodon attacks to reduce the probability of sloops flipping or temporarily capsizing
  • Fixed Greymarrow's ghost to consistently take damage when hit in the head
  • Fixed stronghold kegs stored in the crow's nest to not disappear even when left unattended for a long time
