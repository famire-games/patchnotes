Civilization VII Update 1.5.1 Patch Notes

Civ VII Update 1.5.1 has been released. The diplomatic system now includes a Liberation feature, usability has been greatly improved with a unit list and map search panel, and the Great People system has been overhauled from a production queue method to a unique condition unlock method.

Key changes

  • Added 'Liberation' diplomatic feature allowing conquered cities to be returned to their original owner or city-state
  • Introduced 'Unit List' and 'Map Search' UI panels accessible from the top of the minimap
  • Added new Randomized Earth and Donut maps
  • Overhauled Great People to bypass the city production queue and naturally unlock via civilization-specific unique conditions
  • Pause functionality is now available in multiplayer games even when turn timers are enabled
  • Improved map type selection screen with collapsible categories when creating a game

Buffs

  • Activating Abbas units (Al-Jahiz, Ibn Sina, Al-Farghani) now provides 1 free purchase of their respective unique buildings (Animal Hospital, Hospital, Observatory)
  • Siam's Great Person Chakrabongse activation conditions changed to foreign capital/palace and now provides 500 Influence

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where Harriet Tubman's relationship notification incorrectly triggered when declaring formal war after the leader was eliminated
  • Fixed an issue where additional player slots did not appear immediately when creating a game on a Giant map
  • Fixed an issue where players could not skip the first-meet cinematic
  • Fixed various animation delays and text issues for Ashoka and Ibn Battuta
  • Fixed an issue where Edward Teach and Ada Lovelace appeared as unlock conditions for players without the British DLC
