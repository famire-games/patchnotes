August 27 Update Overview

The August 27 update for Girls' Frontline 2: Exilium brings various new limited-time events such as [Chiral Overlap] and login rewards. Additionally, weapon and Doll pickup procurements for OTs-14, Vepley, and Voimastina, alongside new costumes and weapon skins, have been added.

Key changes

  • Hosting multiple limited-time events including [Chiral Overlap], [Frontier Conquest], and [Reverse Cipher]
  • Obtain [Designated Access Permit x10] via the 7-day login reward
  • Procurement events for OTs-14, Vepley, Voimastina, and related elite weapons
  • [Oath of Reunion] and reunion procurement events for returning commanders
  • Obtain Nemesis MOD items and the original frame for the Doll 'Nemesis'
  • Discounted sales for new costumes and weapon skins for OTs-14, Lukay, and Chuhwa
