Don't Starve Together · October 29, 2026

Halloween Event and Hotfix Update

Spooky appearances for Pigmen, Mermfolk, and Bunnymen, along with new decorations, have been added for Halloween Night. Mobility convenience for the Magical Witch's Broom has been improved, and various bugs have been fixed.

Key changes

  • Added spooky appearances for Pigmen, Mermfolk, and Bunnymen appearing during Halloween Night
  • Added 4 new Halloween decorations
  • Added immunity to slipping, sinkholes, and floor Ick for riders of the Magical Witch's Broom
  • Merm King now gives treats on Halloween Night, similar to the Pig King
  • NPC Ancient Fuelweaver can now be unlinked by striking them with the Shadow Thurible
  • Leaf piles can now be burned

Buffs

  • Added immunity to slipping, sinkholes, and floor Ick while riding the Magical Witch's Broom
  • Changed Merm King to provide treats on Halloween Night

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug where some 'Some' and 'More' options in world settings did not work
  • Added missing knockback states for certain birds and Ticoon
  • Fixed a bug where knocked-back creatures failed to land properly in the ocean or sank
  • Fixed a bug where leaf piles did not pop up when swept or hit by explosives
  • Fixed animation bugs for Spiky Carapace skins
