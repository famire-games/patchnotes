Elden Ring Patch 1.17 Deployed

Elden Ring version 1.17 patch has been released. It includes support for the 'Tarnished Pack' DLC scheduled for release on August 28, 2026, as well as balance adjustments and bug fixes for various weapons, sorceries, incantations, and shields.

Key changes

  • Added support for Tarnished Pack DLC content
  • Added new starting class and spectral steed appearance options
  • Increased attack power for throwing weapons and perfume bottles
  • Increased guard boost and reduced weight for shield categories
  • Improved performance for parries and defensive skills
  • Various PvP adjustments and bug fixes for large katanas and more

Buffs

  • Smithscript Dagger / Rellana's Twin Blades: Increased critical strike damage
  • Smithscript Axe, Spear / Impaler's Spear: Reduced stamina consumption and increased power for throwing attacks
  • Carian Sorcery Sword: Intelligence scaling now increases attack power for all damage types
  • Volcanic Perfume Bottle and 4 other perfume bottles: Increased attack power
  • Large Leather Shield and multiple other shields: Increased guard boost and reduced weight
  • Parry, Storm Wall, Thops's Barrier: Improved parry performance
  • Baptism of Stars and some other sorceries and incantations: Increased power or reduced casting time

Nerfs

  • Rakshasa's Greatsword: Increased delay after left-hand equipment before right-hand weapon attacks, and increased damage taken in PvP
  • Rugalea's Roar / Rotten Breath, etc.: Reduced power and stagger duration in PvP

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug where critical strike damage dealt to large enemies with large katana-class weapons was lower than intended
  • Fixed a bug where jump attacks with large katana-class weapons could be parried
  • Fixed a PC version bug where camera controls were unresponsive during mouse dialogue
  • Fixed a PC version keyboard/mouse settings reset error
