Teamfight Tactics Patch 18.4

Teamfight Tactics Patch 18.4 shifts power between traits and units while granting substantial buffs to underperforming 1-cost and 2-cost units. It also improves consistency for volatile units, adjusts Guardian Spirits and augment balance, and resolves numerous bugs.

Key changes

  • Combat entry and exit phase durations reduced by 1 second each
  • Adjusted and buffed key trait values such as Bloom, Elderwood, and Swiftshot
  • Massive buffs to underperforming 1-cost and 2-cost reroll units
  • Added mana refund functionality when 5-cost units (Ashe, Draven, etc.) miss their skills
  • Tier changes for Chosen by the Sun and added a new 0-gold Guardian Spirit
  • Massive bug fixes related to Aesthetic Waiting, Golden Item Remover, etc.

Buffs

  • Camille skill damage (3-star/4-star)
  • Kobuko health healing amount
  • Rakan shield absorption
  • Xayah skill damage
  • Varus attack speed
  • Alistar health healing and skill damage
  • Gromp area-of-effect damage and attack damage
  • Dusk Wolf empowered basic attack damage
  • Warwick skill damage and health healing
  • Broodmother basic attack damage and skill damage
  • Rengar skill damage
  • Tristana skill damage
  • Nidalee spell power form skill damage
  • Sivir skill damage
  • Draven bleed damage

Nerfs

  • Veigar skill damage
  • Coven 130-essence reward nerfed
  • Elderwood Emblem armor and magic resistance
  • Crownguard bonus spell power after shield expires
  • Kennen shield absorption
  • Ivern Father of Nature hex damage amp and bonus health
  • Dark Ritual spell power per reward tier

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug where Aesthetic Waiting generated clones of Golden Eggs
  • Fixed an issue where some champions' skills disappeared before dealing damage
  • Fixed a bug where Optimization did not grant Golden Item Removers
  • Temporarily disabled Steel Heart to fix a bug
  • Fixed a bug where Xayah 4-star skill damage was incorrect
