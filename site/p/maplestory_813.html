Client 1.2.419 Update Notice

The MapleStory Client 1.2.419 update has been applied. 'Personal Burning' and the autumn event 'Visitors of the Argo' have begun, alongside the addition of the Epic Dungeon 'Aurum Regis' and various system improvements.

Key changes

  • Personal Burning event underway (growth missions, boss missions, personal monsters, and coin shop)
  • MapleStory autumn event 'Visitors of the Argo' hosted (attendance, event skills, etc.)
  • Added Epic Dungeon: Aurum Regis
  • Overhauled Abilities, Scroll Enhancement, Soul Weapons, progression flow, and boss rewards
  • Adjusted Arcane Symbol enhancement costs, field meso penalties, and field improvements
  • Overhauled Mileage and MVP systems

Buffs

  • Combat support magic effects via Argo's Blessing (increases attack/magic power, boss damage, ignore defense, etc.)
  • Tactical support magic effects via Argo's Blessing (increases Sol Erda acquisition rate, Monster Collection registration chance, etc.)

Bug fixes

  • Revised text related to Personal Flames within the Personal Monster category
  • Added caution text regarding bonus EXP in the Personal Burning category
  • Fixed quest-related errors
  • Fixed item and monster-related errors
  • Fixed map and UI-related errors
