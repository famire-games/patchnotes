Battlefield 6 Server Browser Coming Soon

The Server Browser for Battlefield 6 is arriving earlier than planned, beginning a staged rollout shortly after Phase 2 launches on November 10. Players will be able to browse official and player-hosted servers directly with granular filtering, persistent lobbies, and full progression support.

Key changes

  • Staged rollout begins shortly after Phase 2 launches on November 10
  • Access the Server Browser directly within the Quickplay row menu
  • Unified browsing for both Official Studios and Player-hosted servers
  • Granular filtering and sorting by map, mode, ping, and player count
  • Full XP, weapon mastery, and challenge progression on all official servers
  • Persistent lobbies allowing squads to stay together across consecutive rounds
