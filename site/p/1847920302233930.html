Where Winds Meet · October 29, 2026

Version 2.3 Patch Notes and Optimizations

The October 29 update for Where Winds Meet includes fixes for the Guild War timing issue, adjustments to the Battle Pass schedule, and various bug fixes for items, hairstyles, and quests. Additionally, several accessory and title issues have been resolved across all servers.

Key changes

  • Fixed accessory [Autumn Eve Whimsy] and hairstyle [Pristine Grace] display/equipping bugs
  • Rolled back abnormal Guild War scores and sent compensation to affected players
  • Adjusted Battle Pass [Scarlet Whispers] end time and weekly EXP cap reset time
  • Optimized activation conditions for certain Guild Techniques
  • Fixed unclaimable mail, broken quests, and unusable titles

Bug fixes

  • Fixed issue where [Autumn Eve Whimsy] could not be equipped and caused Wardrobe issues
  • Fixed missing [Autumn Eve Whimsy] in the Shop for certain players
  • Fixed PS5 issue preventing dialogue completion in Campaign [Mount Tai Blizzard]
  • Fixed mail reward re-claiming and deletion issues
  • Fixed unactivatable titles [Blooming Rebuttal], [Unrivaled Cardmaster], and [Cast the Cosmos]
