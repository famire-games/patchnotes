Soulmask 1.0 Update Patch #25

Soulmask 1.0 Update Patch #25 has been released. This update requires a client update and includes adjustments to barbarian barracks, elite bosses, tech tree requirements, and combat deployment of wild donkeys alongside various bug fixes.

Key changes

  • Removed Day of the Dead skin states from low-level Barbarian Barracks
  • Removed Day of the Dead special abilities from elite bosses
  • Adjusted production base and windmill tech tree requirements to Level 2
  • Wild donkeys can now be deployed in combat

Buffs

  • Wild donkeys can now be deployed in combat.

Nerfs

  • Removed Day of the Dead skin states from low-level Barbarian Barracks
  • Removed Day of the Dead special abilities from elite bosses

Bug fixes

  • Fixed domesticated alpacas becoming stuck in floors and foundations
  • Fixed feces on Glorious Hymn not clearing when entering water
  • Fixed abnormal issues with Red Crystal configurations
  • Fixed players sometimes being unable to manually use insect repellent or herbicide
