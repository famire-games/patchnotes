Phantom Blade Zero Patch Notes (Version 1.0304629)

Version 1.0304629 update for Phantom Blade Zero has been released. This patch introduces combat assist options, mouse lock-on sensitivity adjustments, memory usage optimizations, and numerous bug fixes and balance corrections.

Key changes

  • Added Combat Assist settings and Automatic Strong Camera Assist option
  • Added Mouse Lock-On Target Switching Sensitivity option
  • Reduced video and system memory usage, especially in cutscenes and snowy areas
  • Updated Soft Snake Sword controls and effects (Hold Execute for Sword Blossom Shield)
  • Improved movement over small rocks, low steps, and gentle slopes
  • Fixed graphics artifacts on AMD GPUs and various quest/combat progression bugs

Buffs

  • Increased the invulnerability window for sidesteps in Guard Stance

Nerfs

  • Fixed Earthshaking Slam skill hitting repeatedly and incorrectly stacking damage
  • Fixed Dance Fan accessory's damage bonus incorrectly applying to and stacking on all skills
  • Fixed Nuo Mask accessory incorrectly reducing Remnant Spark cost for Artifice-type Phantom Edges

Bug fixes

  • Fixed bright square artifacts in volumetric clouds on AMD Radeon RX 9000 Series
  • Fixed an issue where reset enemies could remain invisible while moving and attacking
  • Fixed bosses stopping action during combat under certain conditions
  • Fixed progression blockers and quest reward issues, including the 'Defy Destiny' achievement
  • Fixed issues preventing interaction with A-Ping and her husband in the Village of Lu
