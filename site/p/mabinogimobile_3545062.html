September 17th (Thu) Update Notes

The September 17th update for Mabinogi Mobile introduced new content 'Nightmare Drill', and expanded high-difficulty options such as 'Very Hard' and 'Hell 3' for the field boss Droch Fhinn, Eirel Raid, and Lunda Abyss. Various convenience systems and events such as AI Connector (Beta), Level Skip Tickets, Memory Imprint Presets, and Guild Missions were also introduced.

Key changes

  • Added 'Very Hard' difficulty for field boss Droch Fhinn
  • Added new solo content 'Nightmare Drill' in Gairech Hill
  • Added 'Very Hard' difficulty for Eirel Raid and 'Hell 3' difficulty for Lunda Abyss
  • Added PC version-exclusive 'Mabinogi Mobile AI Connector (Beta)'
  • Added Level 55 and Level 85 Skip Tickets
  • Added Memory Imprint Preset feature and Recommended Activities tab
