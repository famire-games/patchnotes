December 23, 2025 Update Notice

New character 'Lilith', a large batch of new cards, and map skins have been added, alongside the launch of Season 26SP1 'Whispering Shadows'. Additionally, the season transition maintenance method has been changed to zero-downtime maintenance.

Key changes

  • Changed season transition maintenance to zero-downtime maintenance
  • Added new Manifestation character 'Lilith'
  • Added winter festival-themed map skins and escort map 'Lava Factory'
  • Added a large batch of new cards and optimized existing cards
  • Launch of Season 26SP1 'Whispering Shadows' with related season pass and rewards

Buffs

  • Darkthorn Empress: Active skill self-healing amount increased from 20% to 40%
  • Manifestation characters: Added immunity to Stun effects

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an error displayed in Escape mode when there were no refreshable cards for the Superstring faction
  • Fixed incorrect spawning of supply crates and evolution crystals at the start of rounds in Escape mode
  • Fixed incorrect collision box volume when batch-generating complex building clusters
