Client 1.2.419 Update (Skill Adjustments)

Overall skill adjustments and overhauls were introduced through the MapleStory Client 1.2.419 update. Skill effects, damage, and visuals for common and Explorer job classes (Hero, Paladin, Dark Knight, Arch Mage (Fire, Poison), etc.) were heavily modified.

Key changes

  • Added a feature displaying special core cooldowns on buff icons
  • Reduced Abnormal Status resistance duration to 81 seconds
  • Overhauled skill structures and damage for Explorer Warrior and Magician job branches
  • Changed several 5th and 6th job common skills
  • Added new skill 'Erda Permeation'
  • Reduced Overload Mana cooldown to 0.5 seconds

Buffs

  • Increased Empirical Knowledge weakness detection probability to 76% at master level
  • Increased Trance duration from 5 seconds to 10 seconds
  • Reduced Overload Mana cooldown from 30 seconds to 0.5 seconds
  • Buffed Paladin's Final Attack damage
  • Buffed Dark Knight's Pierce Cyclone damage

Nerfs

  • Reduced Flow of Battle damage increase to 15% and removed stacking mechanic
  • Removed Trance stacking mechanic
  • Removed Pirate Flag's party member stats and ignore defense buff functions
  • Removed Epic Adventure's Explorer party member damage buff function
  • Removed Beholder's Buff magic power and party member buff functions

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where Luna Petite skills could be used in the Meisterville gathering farm map
  • Fixed an issue where Luna Petite skills abnormally persisted when unlinking world-shared pet slots
  • Fixed an issue where skill effect transparency options were not applied to certain skill effects
  • Fixed an issue where Mechanic's Crest of the Solar attack damage was incorrectly applied to some enemies
  • Fixed an issue where Overload Mana deactivated when MP reached 0
