September 17 Update Overview

The September 17th update for Girls' Frontline 2: Exilium introduced new limited-time events, Doll and Weapon Pick-up banners, as well as new costumes and weapon skins. Players can obtain Collapse Pieces and cultivation materials by participating in various events.

Key changes

  • Hosted multiple limited-time events including Voyage Logs
  • Increased pick-up rates for Dolls Sopo, Lorelei, and Alba, along with designated weapons
  • Released new costumes for Springfield, Lewis, Sopo, and Alba
  • Discounted sales for the new weapon skin 'Coldsteel Accolade'
  • Provided 2x benefits and rewards for certain limited-time events
