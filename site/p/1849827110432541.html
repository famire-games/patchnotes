Dead by Daylight: The Terrifier PTB Live Update

The toy powers and add-ons of the new Killer 'The Terrifier' have been adjusted based on PTB feedback. Performance metrics such as the bicycle horn, handgun, and tortured status have been modified, and some add-ons have been buffed or nerfed.

Key changes

  • Increased detection radius for the bicycle horn
  • Decreased throw speed and increased duration of the acid pool for the acid bottle
  • Increased movement speed when aiming the handgun
  • Increased duration of the tortured status and inflicted the oblivious status when downing a survivor tortured with the Max Lethality Toy
  • Completely reworked Brooke's Pustule add-on
  • Nerfed Duct Tape and Explosive Gift add-on values, buffed Jonathan's Skull add-on values

Buffs

  • Increased bicycle horn detection radius
  • Increased movement speed when aiming the handgun
  • Increased tortured status duration
  • Buffed Jonathan's Skull add-on effects

Nerfs

  • Decreased acid bottle throw speed
  • Nerfed Duct Tape add-on values
  • Nerfed Explosive Gift add-on values
