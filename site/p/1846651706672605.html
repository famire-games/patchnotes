Ascended Teros & 11th Anniversary Event – Patch 11.00

Celebrate Brawlhalla's 11th Anniversary with a Gala event hosted by Vector, featuring the new Ascended Teros Epic Skin and event rewards. This update also delivers final balance adjustments ahead of BCX 2026, experimental weapon spawn changes, and Qinghua & Baobao's tournament debut.

Key changes

  • 11th Anniversary Gala Event hosted by Vector with exclusive rewards
  • New Ascended Teros Epic Skin bundle and thematic items
  • New Elysium Brawlball map and experimental weapon spawn improvements
  • First balance pass for Qinghua & Baobao, making them legal for official tournaments
  • Weapon balance updates for Katars, Blasters, Chakram, and Hammer
  • Legend balance adjustments for Ada, Caspian, Gnash, Mako, Qinghua & Baobao, Sentinel, Teros, and Zariel

Buffs

  • Katars Neutral Light total damage increased
  • Hammer Side Light and Down Light damage increased
  • Gnash Neutral Hammer minimum charge time decreased
  • Mako Side and Down Greatsword damage and utility enhanced
  • Sentinel Neutral Katars and Neutral Hammer recover time and momentum improved

Nerfs

  • Katars Side Light, Down Light, and Down Air damage decreased
  • Blasters Side Light recovery on miss increased and Down Light damage decreased
  • Chakram backwards acceleration and max speed on miss decreased
  • Ada Side and Down Spear force and damage reduced
  • Zariel Neutral Bow and Down Gauntlets force and damage reduced

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue with Qinghua & Baobao Side Cannon where targets could be dropped before the final hit
  • Decreased Teros Side Axe maximum charge time to fix an animation bug
