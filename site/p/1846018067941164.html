Satisfactory v1.2.4.1 Update Fixes

The Satisfactory v1.2.4.1 patch re-enables audio routing to fix audio-related mods, updates the Wwise version, and resolves several important crashes and bugs. Key fixes include preventing signs from being wiped upon loading and fixing console audio freezes.

Key changes

  • Re-enabled Audio Routing and Metasound for audio mods
  • Updated internal Wwise version to 2025.1.6
  • Restored Engine.ini to original status

Bug fixes

  • Fixed signs around a Pioneer getting wiped blank upon loading a save
  • Fixed Audio/WWise related freeze on Consoles
  • Fixed crash when aiming at a Conveyor Belt with dismantled Throughput Monitors
  • Fixed Dedicated Server crash when placing a Conveyor Throughput Monitor
  • Fixed audio-related crash when exiting the game
