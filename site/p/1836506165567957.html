Frostpunk 2 Hotfix 1.6.1 Patch Notes

Frostpunk 2 Hotfix 1.6.1 addresses balance issues, crashes, and various audio, UI, and general bugs related to the Breach of Trust update. Key changes include adjusted DLC2 scenario parameters, lower weapon costs for resistance abilities, and enhanced controller navigation.

Key changes

  • Adjusted global parameters for DLC2 and weather variance for easier difficulties
  • Lowered weapon costs for resistance abilities (Enforce Military Zone to 50, Force Takeover to 120)
  • Added additional coal outposts and increased starting prefabs to 130
  • Resolved boot, runtime, and subsystem crashes
  • Fixed gamepad navigation issues including Generator Relocation Law access
  • Added a Skip Battle button and improved audio/visual fidelity

Buffs

  • Reduced weapon costs for Enforce Military Zone (from 75 to 50) and Force Takeover (from 200 to 120)
  • Altering the Deal message now grants an additional 50k Food
  • Increased starting materials, goods, and prefabs (from 30 to 130)

Bug fixes

  • Fixed deployment base placement issues on non-frostbroken areas
  • Fixed an issue preventing optional objectives when reloading saves with active Food New Edinburgh quest
  • Fixed volcano tile spawning in Utopia Mode and skyrocketing population growth
  • Fixed controller focus issues for Generator Relocation Law and sliders
  • Corrected localized store hyperlink routing for DLCs
