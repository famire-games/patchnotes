Call of Duty: Warzone Season 1 Overview

Call of Duty: Warzone Season 1 begins with a full integration with Modern Warfare 4. A new Korea-themed battle royale map 'Hajin' and Resurgence map 'Zodiac' are added, along with player level resets and various new equipment and gameplay adjustments.

Key changes

  • Full integration with Modern Warfare 4 and player level reset
  • Added new battle royale map 'Hajin' (Korea-themed)
  • Added new resurgence map 'Zodiac'
  • Made tactical sprint enabled by default and added dragging allies while reviving
  • Introduced new equipment and tactical gear such as the Zipline Launcher called Home
  • Verdansk and Rebirth Island scheduled to return mid-season

Buffs

  • All operators can use Tactical Sprint without perks

Nerfs

  • Tactical Sprint duration slightly shortened

Bug fixes

  • Improved visibility and readability across all maps
  • Improved listening convenience through better footsteps audio
