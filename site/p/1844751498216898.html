Rocket League Season 24 Patch Notes v2.76

Rocket League Season 24 begins on September 23, 2026, introducing new content such as Honor Duels, Black Market Trade-In, and a new Bullet Ball LTM. Additionally, various feature improvements like custom scoreboards, modernized keyboard and mouse controls, and several bug fixes have been implemented.

Key changes

  • Added Honor Duels allowing players to challenge other players to a 1v1 during a match
  • Trade in 3 duplicate Black Market items to acquire a new Black Market item
  • Match MVP's equipped Goal Explosion now plays on the podium screen with their name displayed
  • Added new Bullet Ball LTM where the last player standing wins
  • Added a feature where specific titles track and display certain stats and unlock new visual effects based on milestones
  • Added custom scoreboards allowing players to select stats to display on the scoreboard per game mode

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where the client would freeze on the previous frame when Alt-Tabbing during resolution changes on PC
  • Fixed an issue where the front end was invisible when booting Rocket League after waking from sleep mode on PS5
  • Fixed a crash that could occur upon console boot due to missing packages
  • Fixed a crash that occurred in the main menu preview
  • Fixed an issue where the SMAA anti-aliasing option failed to smooth rough edges on PC
