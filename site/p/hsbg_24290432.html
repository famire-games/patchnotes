Hearthstone Battlegrounds Patch 36.2

Battlegrounds Season 14 'Dalaran's Dark Gifts' is here, introducing the Dark Gifts system, the 'Spend' keyword, Vault and Fish Bait mechanics, trinkets, and a massive minion refresh. Additionally, seven heroes have been buffed, the Venomous keyword was updated, and stat readability for numbers over 4 digits was improved.

Key changes

  • Added 'Dark Gifts' system to discover gifts using Gold
  • Added 'Spend' keyword to trigger special minion abilities during Recruits
  • Introduced Pirate 'Vault' and Beast 'Fish Bait' synergies
  • Trinkets retained alongside a massive minion and tavern spell overhaul
  • Updated Venomous keyword and Chromatic Drake, plus 4+ digit number compression
  • Season 14 Battlegrounds Track and new heroes/cosmetics added

Buffs

  • Karim Rom: Hero power cost 1 -> 0
  • Anodized Robo Cub: Hero power triggers twice (Wait, Anodized/Enhance-o: Enhance-o Mechano triggers twice)
  • Edwin VanCleef: 5 cards -> 4 cards to upgrade
  • Overlord Saurfang: 4 minions -> 3 minions to improve
  • Ragnaros the Firelord: 16 cards -> 12 cards for Sulfuras
  • Lich Baz'hial (Rakanishu): 4 turns -> 3 turns to upgrade (Rakanishu)
  • Tethered Bloodwatcher (Tetheran): 4th -> 3rd tavern spell costs (0)
