Version 80: Blooming Update

This update includes new and returning forest moon creatures, the introduction of a 'Utility Slot' exclusively for store-bought tools, new rooms inside facilities, an exterior rework of planets, and massive performance optimizations and bug fixes.

Key changes

  • Added a dedicated utility slot for purchased tools (excluding certain items like shovels)
  • Added 3 new creatures to the forest moon (Backwater Gunkfish, Feiopars, Cadaver Bloom) and re-added the Kidnapper Fox
  • Completely reworked the exterior areas of the March moon and updated vegetation on Vow and Adamance
  • Changed main shaders to improve rendering performance and enhance terrain/vegetation rendering
  • Reduced lag spikes through memory allocation optimization and creature AI memory sharing
  • Added new accessibility options such as turning off head bobbing while running and sprint toggle

Buffs

  • Relieved inventory pressure by adding a dedicated utility slot for exclusive tools
  • Scrap can now spawn even at the dead ends of general factory interior corridors
  • Made scrap exploration easier by adding cave pockets inside mines
  • Increased the time it takes for players to drown in swimming pools and other bodies of water by about 1 second

Nerfs

  • Slightly decreased night vision range, making it more dangerous to navigate the dark without light sources
  • Old Birds can now walk over and smash through legs when walking on top of them

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where the payout UI could not properly scroll through all sold scrap
  • Fixed an issue where exterior creatures could attack or deal damage to players while elevators were closed
  • Fixed an issue where player hands and fingers did not align with the lever when pulling the landing lever
  • Fixed corporate ads displaying inverted discount rates between clients and hosts
  • Fixed Hygrodere failing to deform or change size according to the environment
