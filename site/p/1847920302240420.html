Deadlock · October 30, 2026

Halloween 2026 and Gameplay Update

Halloween themes and an autumn festival event have been added to Deadlock, along with a Mid Boss revamp, objective and hero balance adjustments. Halloween virtual heroes and Manhattan streets now feature cauldrons to earn candy.

Key changes

  • Halloween event running until November 2nd and cauldron candy system added
  • Mid Boss health increased, reward structure changed, and Rejuvenator Crystal drops scattered
  • Adjusted Haunted Soul and comeback gold mechanisms
  • Recently released 6 heroes 'New' classification removed and enabled for ranked bans
  • Buffs/nerfs to items (such as Shadow Weave) and multiple heroes

Buffs

  • Shadow Weave: Added +50% Embassy weapon damage
  • Toxic Bullets: Anti-heal increased from 35% to 40%
  • Battle Vest: Weapon damage increased from 18% to 22%
  • Abrams: Base gun damage increased by 5%
  • Kelvin: Added -20% enemy fire rate reduction effect to Frozen Shelter T2

Nerfs

  • High-Velocity Rounds: Bullet velocity bonus reduced from 60% to 45%
  • Express Shot: Secondary fire weapon damage reduced by 5%
  • Spirit Sap: Cooldown increased from 18s to 22s
  • Graves: Movement speed reduced from 7 to 6.7
  • Celeste: Shining Wonder damage reduced from 140 to 125

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug where some chests had delayed respawns while other chests or power-up pickups remained
  • Fixed bugs related to Walker stomp logic
  • Fixed an issue where Apollo's repost counter-assist dash missed or bypassed targets
  • Fixed a bug where Deadman Danny's Chimera Mk1 dealt no damage during melee attacks
  • Fixed an issue where damage dealt to barriers in Focus Lens was not counted towards periodic damage
