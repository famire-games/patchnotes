Deadlock Minor Patch - November 4, 2026

A Deadlock minor patch has been released, buffing heroes such as Apollo, Mo & Krill, Nurse Harrow, and Rat King, while nerfing Healbane, Baba, Celeste, and Deadman Danny. Hero skill values and item effects have also been adjusted.

Key changes

  • Healbane stack reduction effect made more aggressive
  • Apollo's Riposte and Itani Lo Sahn buffed
  • Overall nerfs to Baba's Spirit resistance, melee damage, and Brew cooldown
  • Celeste's skill range, silence duration, and slow effect nerfed
  • Mo & Krill's Scorn heal amount, Burrow radius, and combo reward buffed
  • Nurse Harrow and Rat King's base bullet and skill stats buffed

Buffs

  • Apollo: Riposte - Pommel Hit now also disarms the enemy
  • Apollo: Riposte - Pummel hit now ends any preexisting parry on the target
  • Apollo: Riposte - T2 increased from +0.6s duration to +0.7s
  • Apollo: Itani Lo Sahn - T3 bonus damage increased from +50% to +60%
  • Mo & Krill: Scorn - Heal from heroes increased from 1.2x to 1.3x
  • Mo & Krill: Burrow - Damage radius increased by +0.5m
  • Mo & Krill: Sand Blast - T2 slow from -24% Slow and -26% Dash distance to -28% for both
  • Mo & Krill: Combo - Health per kill increased from 40 to 50
  • Nurse Harrow: Bullet damage increased by 8%
  • Nurse Harrow: Lurking Terrors - Vertical angle increased from 50% to 65%
  • Nurse Harrow: Lurking Terrors - Fear buildup time reduced from 2s to 1.75s
  • Rat King: Bullet damage increased from 5.7 to 5.8
  • Rat King: Ammo increased from 24 to 26
  • Rat King: Rule, Ratannia! - Move Speed increased from +3m to +4m
  • Rat King: Rule, Ratannia! - T1 now also reduces cooldown by -25s

Nerfs

  • Healbane: Stacks now diminish 20% more aggressively
  • Baba: Spirit Resistance scaling reduced from 0.7 to 0.5
  • Baba: Melee damage per boon reduced by 10%
  • Baba: Baba's Brew - Cooldown increased from 27s to 28s
  • Celeste: Dazzling Trick - T3 silence duration reduced from +1.25s to +0.75s
  • Deadman Danny: Close The Deal - Bonus damage to under half health targets reduced from +75% to +55%
