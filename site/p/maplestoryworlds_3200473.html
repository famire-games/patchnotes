MapleStory Worlds · October 29, 2025

October 30 (Thu) Update Patch Notes

A MapleStory Worlds update took place on Thursday, October 30. The voice chat feature during world play has been removed, and the friend deletion function in the social panel along with Nexon OTP PC designated device settings have been added. Additionally, an issue where console logs were displayed in certain worlds has been fixed.

Key changes

  • Removed voice chat feature during world play
  • Added friend deletion function to the social panel
  • Added Nexon OTP PC designated device setting feature
  • Fixed an issue where console logs were displayed to players in certain worlds

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where console logs were displayed to players during world play in certain worlds
