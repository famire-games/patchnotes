Honkai: Star Rail · December 16, 2025

Version 3.8 Update "Memory is a Dream's Opening"

The Honkai: Star Rail Version 3.8 update is live, introducing a new Trailblaze Mission, 5-star character 'Dahlia', new Light Cones, and events. Additionally, major adjustments were made to Currency Wars and Divergent Universe, alongside various system quality-of-life updates and bug fixes.

Key changes

  • New Penacony Trailblaze Mission "Memory is a Dream's Opening" unlocked
  • Added new 5-star character "Dahlia (Nihility: Fire)" and new 5-star Light Cone
  • Hosted new events "Golden Meow Championship" and "Sunset's Remnant"
  • Large-scale adjustments and updates to "Currency Wars: Zero-Sum Game"
  • Added quick Path switching and preset saving functions to the team formation screen
  • Optimized mobile connection performance by adding a shader compilation option upon login

Bug fixes

  • Fixed abnormal skill and effect behavior for characters such as Cyrene, Dan Heng • Imbibitor Lunae, and Ceryx under specific circumstances
  • Fixed upper limit errors for bonds and stacks of certain characters like Hyacine, Jade, and Feixiao in Currency Wars
  • Fixed abnormal animation and combat perspective issues during Divergent Universe and mission progression
  • Fixed an issue where the achievement "Just Trust Dan Heng!" could not be unlocked
  • Fixed and optimized multilingual dialogue, books, and system guides, including Korean voice lines and text
