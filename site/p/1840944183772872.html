v1.0.3 Patch Notes: Balance Adjustments and Bug Fixes

With the Palworld v1.0.3 patch, level and material requirements for Jetragon-related technologies and the Underwater Construction Kit have been eased. Additionally, Crude Oil Extractor production speed has been increased, World Tree Holy Water adjustments have been made, and various multiplayer and in-game bugs have been fixed.

Key changes

  • Lowered unlock level and reduced crafting materials for Jetragon-related technologies
  • Changed the Underwater Construction Kit unlock level from 66 to 23
  • Reduced construction costs for Crude Oil Extractors and Large Power Generators while increasing crude oil production speed
  • Doubled the fiber yield obtained from trees
  • Extended duration and reduced weight of World Tree Holy Water
  • Added a server setting option to disable the restriction on enemy camp spawns near bases

Buffs

  • Lowered tech unlock level for Jetragon equipment (Level 79 -> Level 70) and reduced materials
  • Lowered tech unlock level for Underwater Construction Kit (Level 66 -> Level 23) and reduced points required
  • Extended duration and reduced weight of World Tree Holy Water (1 -> 0.1)
  • Doubled fiber yield from trees
  • Reduced construction costs for Crude Oil Extractors and Large Power Generators
  • Increased crude oil production speed for Crude Oil Extractors and High-Pressure Crude Oil Extractors

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where accepting the main mission after removing the barrier in Sunreach would make progression impossible
  • Fixed an issue where fast travel was unintentionally enabled during tower boss battles on dedicated servers
  • Fixed an issue where summoned Pals would stop combat or base tasks
  • Fixed an issue where Alpha Serpentis did not produce leather when placed in a ranch
  • Fixed an issue where items were duplicated under specific circumstances
