Modern Warfare 4 Season 1 Battle Pass and BlackCell Overview

Here are the details for the Modern Warfare 4 Season 1 Battle Pass, BlackCell, and the new store bundle system. An auto-catch-up system guaranteeing over 50 rewards, along with BlackCell-exclusive perks, challenges, and rewards, have been added.

Key changes

  • Season 1 Battle Pass and BlackCell contents offering over 100 rewards
  • Introduced an auto-catch-up system for delayed players (automatically earn rewards up to day 50)
  • Immediate grants upon purchasing BlackCell including XERO operator, 1,100 COD Points, and 20 Tier Skip Tokens
  • Earn XP and advance tiers by playing Multiplayer, DMZ, and Warzone
  • Added BlackCell-exclusive perks in DMZ mode (additional vendor slots, daily rewards, FOB visual upgrades, etc.)
  • Added in-game challenges to unlock BlackCell-exclusive weapon camos and operator skins
