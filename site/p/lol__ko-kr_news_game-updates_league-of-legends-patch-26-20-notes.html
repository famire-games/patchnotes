League of Legends Patch Notes 26.20

League of Legends Patch 26.20 focuses on pro-play-oriented champion adjustments for the World Championship, including buffs to Kennen and Kindred. Additionally, Aatrox, Caitlyn, Irelia, Karma, and Quinn join the Classic mode, alongside various item and scoreboard UI changes, and bug fixes.

Key changes

  • Pro-play-focused nerfs to Ambessa, Ashe, Cassiopeia, K'Sante, Yunara, etc.
  • Buffs to Diana, Kennen, Kindred, Lillia, Lucian, Mordekaiser, Neeko, Smolder, Swain, Tahm Kench, Vayne
  • Added 5 champions to Classic mode: Aatrox, Caitlyn, Irelia, Karma, Quinn
  • Item adjustments including Hextech Rocketbelt and Experimental Hexplate
  • Hidden item count numbers on the scoreboard for most items excluding strategically vital ones
  • Extended 5-player ranked queue operation hours to 16:00 ~ 4:00

Buffs

  • Diana W bonus health ratio increased
  • Kennen E bonus attack speed increased
  • Kindred W monster damage ratio and E slow duration increased
  • Lillia R duration increased
  • Lucian growth attack damage increased
  • Mordekaiser Q late-game damage increased and R cooldown decreased
  • Neeko E base damage and empowered root duration increased
  • Smolder Q, E, and R critical strike and healing ratios increased
  • Swain base magic resistance, W damage, and cooldown increased
  • Tahm Kench Q base healing increased
  • Vayne health regeneration per 5 seconds and Q mana cost adjusted

Nerfs

  • Ambessa P damage decreased
  • Ashe growth attack damage decreased
  • Cassiopeia base health and mana decreased
  • K'Sante W mana cost and damage reduction decreased
  • Yunara Q on-hit damage decreased and W slow stacking removed
  • Experimental Hexplate ranged efficiency decreased
  • Hextech Rocketbelt ability haste decreased and damage adjusted
  • Runaan's Hurricane damage per bolt decreased
  • Fiora W bonus attack damage decreased
  • Graves base attack damage, growth stats, and R damage decreased
  • Fizz Q and W AP ratios decreased

Bug fixes

  • Fixed Nautilus P empowered basic attack critical strike damage bug and W shield/DoT bugs
  • Fixed multiple bugs related to projectile dispersion augments upon jumping or casting skills
  • Fixed various bugs in Classic and Normal modes including champion skills, splash arts, and tooltip errors
