Patch 36.2.2 Notes (Battlegrounds)

Hearthstone Battlegrounds Patch 36.2.2 has been released. The overperforming 'Tasty Lobster' has been nerfed, while the underperforming Quilboar minion type alongside numerous heroes, minions, spells, trinkets, and Gifts of the Dark have received major adjustments.

Key changes

  • Tasty Lobster's Deathrattle effect reduced to a single random friendly Beast
  • Major Quilboar minion type rework (including a Tier 7 buff for Recidivist Stormtrooper)
  • Changed Inge the Iron Hymn's Hero Power and adjusted Lord Barov's Armor
  • Removed many Lesser and Greater Trinkets, and adjusted costs and text
  • Adjusted balance and appearance timing for Gifts of the Dark

Buffs

  • Multi-tribe Alliance stats and buff increased (+3/+3)
  • Tyrande stats and target stats increased (50/50)
  • Last Man Standing stats and rally effect increased (+15/+15)
  • Champion of Sargeras Tavern minion buff increased (+8/+8)
  • Stone Age Tablet stats and effect increased (+20/+20)

Nerfs

  • Tasty Lobster Deathrattle target reduced (two Beasts -> one random Beast)
  • Lord Barov Armor reduced (12 -> 6)
  • Constructbot stats changed (5/1 -> 2/1)
  • Seafaring Extortionist scaling reduced (8/8 -> 7/7)
  • Corruptedrusher Butcher Vengeance requirement increased (3 -> 4)

Bug fixes

  • Removed lingering Gift of the Dark radiance visual effects after stats were doubled by Diplopia, Gilded, or Cultivation
  • Slightly shortened the turn timer in Solobattle
  • Banned all Gifts of the Dark from the Rattling Box pool
