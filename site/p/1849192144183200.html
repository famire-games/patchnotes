Version 2.4 Path Balance Adjustment Announcement

The Where Winds Meet Version 2.4 update brings progression optimizations, path and martial arts balance adjustments, and quality-of-life features. Key updates include modulated gear recovery, free set switching, and catch-up mechanics for leveling and gear.

Key changes

  • Added 'Modulated Gear Recovery' and source filter options for gear recovery
  • Introduced 'Free Unlocked Set Switching' without consuming Echo Jade
  • Optimized initial affixes and tuning drop rates for Tier 100 gear
  • Expanded battle build saving to include gear attuning and mystic skill settings
  • Adjusted balance and talents for various martial arts and inner ways

Buffs

  • Increased appearance rates for rare affixes in Tier 100 tuning libraries
  • Enhanced damage multipliers and effects for specific martial arts and inner ways

Nerfs

  • Adjusted immediate endurance recovery and damage reduction values for certain inner ways
