Marvel Rivals Patch Notes - November 26, 2026

A server-maintenance-free patch deployed on November 26, 2026, introducing the new 18v18 map 'Incursion Point' and the 2nd-anniversary festival 'Rivals Day 2026'. Various shop items, Ghostworks Arcade minigames, and the Black Friday sale have begun, alongside hero and map bug fixes.

Key changes

  • Added new 18v18 map 'Incursion Point' (excluding PS4)
  • Kicked off the Marvel Rivals 2nd-anniversary festival 'Rivals Day 2026'
  • Added Ghostworks Arcade minigames and rewards
  • Van Dine's Idea Matrix event and free legendary Deadpool costume provided
  • Black Friday sale with up to 40% discounts
  • New shop bundles and chromas for Lady Loki, Scarlet Witch, Moon Knight, etc.

Bug fixes

  • Groot: Fixed an issue where attacking certain map obstacles unintentionally triggered wall awakening effects
  • Moon Knight: Fixed an issue where the Blood Moon Trap Team-Up ability's Talon of Khonshu failed to deal damage to map energy barriers
  • Loki: Fixed abnormally high ultimate charge efficiency when transforming via the Villain's Illusion Team-Up ability
  • Maps: Fixed characters getting stuck in abnormal locations due to revives and movement abilities in Museum of Conflagration and other maps
  • Other: Fixed graphical and audio bugs including PS5 spray preview screen control errors, Wolverine costume, and Winter Soldier sounds
