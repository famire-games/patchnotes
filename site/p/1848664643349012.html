Foundry Server Guild War Update Notice

Guild Wars are coming to the Epic Seven Foundry server, complete with schedules, defense team setup rules, and battle regulations. To celebrate, special rewards will be distributed to all Foundry server users.

Key changes

  • Foundry Server Guild War update (Defense team placement on Nov 21, matchmaking begins Nov 23)
  • Battle mode where you compete with guild members against other guilds to earn destruction points
  • Up to 2 defense teams consisting of 3 members each can be deployed per guild member
  • Matchmaking between Foundry server guilds takes place 3 times a week
  • Special rewards distributed to all logged-in Foundry server users (1,000 Proof of Valor, 3,000 Crest of Courage)
