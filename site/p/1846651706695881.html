Judge of Death - October Update

A new Legion Raid-tier content, 'The Judge of Death Velgardin', has been added alongside new combat trials, a reward track, and a new equipment slot, 'Vambraces'. Additionally, Tier 4 endgame content has been expanded with new tiers for Chaos Rift, Shade of the Guardian, and Haal's Hourglass, as well as higher-tier Chaos Gates.

Key changes

  • Added new Shadow Raid 'The Judge of Death Velgardin'
  • Introduced new combat trials and reward tracks
  • Added new equipment slot 'Vambraces'
  • Expanded Tier 4 endgame content (new tiers for Chaos Rift, Shade of the Guardian, and Haal's Hourglass)
  • Added new Chaos Gate tiers for advanced adventurers
