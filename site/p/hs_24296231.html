Patch 36.4.2 Notes

This 36.4.2 patch brings balance updates to Hearthstone, Arena, and Battlegrounds. In Hearthstone, certain cards have been adjusted to curb problematic meta trends, while Arena class balance and card appearance rates have been adjusted.

Key changes

  • Small-scale balance adjustments in Hearthstone to promote meta stability
  • Stat adjustments for Abrasive Buccaneer, Gigantfin, Quake-Foot, and more
  • Decreased Tinyfin stats and increased Forbidden Cipher quest requirement
  • 100% Arcane Dust refund for Food Chain, Tinyfin, and Forbidden Cipher for 2 weeks
  • Adjusted appearance rates for specific classes and cards in Arena
  • Fixed a bug where cards from the Operation: Citadel Escape set did not appear as intended in Arena draft pools

Buffs

  • Aghanim: Increased Attack (8/9 -> 9/9)
  • Scary Behemoth: Changed stats (9/6 -> 8/8)
  • Quake-Foot: Changed Mana cost and stats (9 Mana 9/9 -> 8 Mana 8/8)

Nerfs

  • Tinyfin: Decreased Health (1/3 -> 1/2)
  • Forbidden Cipher: Changed quest requirement (Discover 7 cards -> Discover 8 cards)

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug where cards from the Operation: Citadel Escape set did not appear as intended during the Arena deck building phase
