Patch 26.21 Notes

League of Legends patch 26.21 is now live. Balance adjustments have been made to numerous champions and items, alongside a revamp of the ARAM 'Mayhem' mode, the addition of new champions (Mordekaiser, Yorick, Hecarim, Lucian) to LoL Classic mode, and Arena balance tweaks.

Key changes

  • Buffed Azir, Darius, Miss Fortune, Nunu & Willump, Orianna, Ryze, Sion, Taliyah, Taric, Twisted Fate, Vel'Koz, Xin Zhao, and Zyra
  • Nerfed Caitlyn, Jinx, Lee Sin, Roec, Nidalee, Qiyana, Rammus, Tristana, Varus, Viktor, Wukong, and Yone
  • ARAM Mayhem mode 'Journey to Stardom' and numerous new augments added
  • Mordekaiser, Yorick, Hecarim, and Lucian join LoL Classic mode
  • Massive champion and item balance adjustments in Arena mode
  • Limited replay downloads to 10 times per hour to prevent malicious replay harvesting

Buffs

  • Azir mana growth increased
  • Darius Q cooldown reduced
  • Miss Fortune E and R damage increased
  • Nunu & Willump R maximum damage increased
  • Orianna Q and W damage AP ratios increased
  • Ryze Q and W stats increased
  • Sion health and R damage increased
  • Taliyah E damage increased
  • Taric can now cast abilities on untargetable allies
  • Twisted Fate R vision acquisition improved
  • Vel'Koz passive damage increased
  • Xin Zhao Q and E ratios increased
  • Zyra W seed recharge time decreased
  • Varus Q ratio and channel reward buffed
  • Dawncore AP grant increased

Nerfs

  • Caitlyn Headshot and Q damage adjusted
  • Jinx base attack damage and health reduced
  • Lee Sin W cooldown increased and R damage reduced
  • Roec mana and health restoration adjusted
  • Nidalee W and E stats reduced
  • Qiyana Q damage reduced
  • Rammus W armor/magic resistance reduced
  • Tristana Q attack speed reduced
  • Varus W on-hit damage adjusted
  • Viktor movement speed and Q shield reduced
  • Wukong health regeneration and Stone Skin reduced
  • Yone Q damage reduced
  • Hullbreaker lethality and shaped charge damage reduced
  • Greed's Greaves price increased
  • Hubris attack damage adjusted

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug regarding Varus Q damage
  • Fixed a bug where Lee Sin E failed to detect stealthed enemies
  • Fixed a Sion R tooltip error
  • Fixed a visual bug with Rammus Q trajectory
  • Fixed a bug displaying Quinn's Valor as a champion
