Cyberpunk 2077 'Edgerunners 2' (2.5) Update

The Cyberpunk 2077 2.5 ('Edgerunners 2') update launches on October 22. It introduces new side quests, outfit sets, radio stations, hand cyberware customization, dual pistol gameplay, and cross-platform mod support.

Key changes

  • New side quests 'Clampdown' and 'Paradise' added
  • New perk 'Double Trouble' allowing Power category pistols and revolvers to be wielded as dual pistols
  • Visit Glamdakk to customize the appearance of hand cyberware such as Mantis Blades and Gorilla Arms
  • Added mod.io-based cross-platform mod support
  • New clothing collections and several hairstyles previously exclusive to NPCs added for V
  • Added DLSS 4.5 support and glasses-free 3D effect support for Samsung Odyssey 3D monitors

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where a Delamain taxi could get stuck after colliding with other vehicles, blocking quest progression
  • Fixed an issue where film grain had no effect when enabled
  • Fixed an issue where V would crouch or stand up on certain control schemes when tagging enemies while scanning
  • Fixed missing 3rd-person camera controller settings
  • Fixed typos in various languages
