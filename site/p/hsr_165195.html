Version 4.4 Update

Honkai: Star Rail Version 4.4 「The Moment Silence Settles, the Horn Sounds」 is now live. This update brings new Trailblaze Missions, Fate collaboration content, new characters including Himeko Nova, Rin Tohsaka, and Gilgamesh, as well as quality-of-life system improvements.

Key changes

  • New Trailblaze Missions and Fate collaboration epilogue/events added
  • New ★5 characters Himeko Nova, Rin Tohsaka, and Gilgamesh added
  • New Light Cones, costumes, and areas added
  • Achievement search and batch claim, plus quick level-up features for characters and Light Cones
  • Major quality-of-life improvements for Relic enhancement and equipping
  • Ornament extraction available without completing Simulated Universe prerequisite missions

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where Tenya Blade's modeling and Ultimate UI expression displayed abnormally under certain circumstances
  • Fixed processing anomalies and abnormal debuff activations during battles with certain enemies
  • Fixed modeling and effect errors occurring during Divergent Universe and field progression
  • Fixed several voice lines and dialogue output errors
  • Fixed controller and UI-related interaction errors
