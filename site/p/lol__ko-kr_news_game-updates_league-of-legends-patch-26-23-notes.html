League of Legends Patch 26.23 Notes

Patch 26.23 welcomes the return of the Winter Map and pilots Team Voice Chat on the North America and Oceania servers. Additionally, champion balance adjustments have been made to bridge the gap between pro play and standard games, alongside balance revamps for ARAM: Mayhem and Arena modes.

Key changes

  • Team Voice Chat pilot introduced for NA and OCE servers
  • Winter Rift map returns and legacy Winter skins available for purchase
  • Buffs to Annie, Azir, Bard, Ezreal, Galio, Gnar, K'Sante, Mel, Vel'Koz, and Vi
  • Nerfs to LeBlanc, Master Yi, and Syndra
  • New Headliners, Augments, and Turret Plating introduced in ARAM: Mayhem
  • Talon, Udyr, Urgot, and Xerath added to Classic mode

Buffs

  • Annie (Tibbers Health, aura damage, and basic attack damage increased)
  • Azir (Base attack range and W on-hit/on-attack ratios increased)
  • Bard (Q damage and AP ratio increased)
  • Ezreal (W and R damage ratios increased)
  • Galio (W shield amount increased)
  • Gnar (Mini/Mega Gnar Q damage ratios increased)
  • K'Sante (W mana cost reduced and base damage increased)
  • Mel (Q and W damage and shield amount increased)
  • Vel'Koz (R total damage slightly increased)
  • Vi (E damage increased)

Nerfs

  • LeBlanc (Q AP ratio decreased)
  • Master Yi (Q damage and maximum single-target damage AD ratios decreased)
  • Syndra (E mana cost and cooldown increased)
  • Stormrazor (Volt unique passive bonus damage decreased)

Bug fixes

  • Restored Swain's basic attack and ability voice lines and corrected visual effects
  • Fixed an issue where Volibear's E was visible through the Fog of War
  • Restored ability voice lines and fixed awkward animations for Elise in Human Form
  • Fixed a bug where Hecarim's E sound effects were missing
  • Fixed a visual effect bug on Yorick's R
