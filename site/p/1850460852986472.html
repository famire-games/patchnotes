Minor Update - 11-26-2026

Hero base health and bonus systems have been adjusted, along with balance changes for creeps, mid-bosses, and hounds. In addition, damage scaling and summon mechanics have been modified, and a bot drill feature has been added to sandbox mode.

Key changes

  • Hero base health decreased by 8% and redistributed to Boons
  • First kill bonus souls increased from 125 to 175
  • Mid-boss health decreased and damage increased by 30%
  • Damage amplification and penalties now uniformly apply to all weapon, melee, and spirit damage
  • Added various hero-specific 'Bot Drill' practice sections to sandbox mode
  • Adjusted performance and cooldowns for certain items (Capacitor, Healing Nova, Dispel Magic, etc.)

Buffs

  • Bullet damage growth per boon increased by 5% for all heroes (with some exceptions)
  • Increased first kill bonus souls (125 -> 175)
  • Floating health snack respawn time shortened and pickup radius increased
  • Hounds ability damage increased by 30% and resistances buffed
  • Permanent buffs (bullet resistance, spirit resistance, ability range, movement speed) buffed
  • Healing Nova spirit scaling increased (1.1 -> 1.25)

Nerfs

  • Hero base health decreased by 8%
  • Comeback souls reduced by approximately 5% across all lanes
  • Trooper damage vs. heroes increased by 10%
  • Hounds bounty reduced by 5%
  • Express Shot secondary weapon damage growth per boon reduced by approx. 10%
  • Sharpshooter falloff range reduced (+20% -> +18%)
  • Capacitor shock damage reduced (43 -> 37)
  • Dispel Magic cooldown increased (45s -> 52s)
  • Cheat Death cooldown increased (90s -> 105s)
  • Spirit Burn debuff duration reduced (8s -> 7s)

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where Hounds briefly took reduced damage while airborne
  • Fixed an issue where bullet and spirit resistance reductions did not properly lower Hounds' resistance
  • Fixed an issue where teleport abilities would intermittently fail to cast when too close to walls
  • Fixed an issue where heroes hiding in steam vents remained untargetable even after enemy heroes entered
  • Fixed Rejuvenator timer rings displaying incorrectly after reconnecting or restarting when picking up a different Rejuvenator
