Patch 2.7.0.5 on the Public Branch

Medieval Dynasty update 2.7.0.5 is now available on the Public Branch, resolving issues related to the recent release. This update includes fixes for Legendary Animal spawning, throwing rock damage, and co-op skill bugs.

Key changes

  • Fixed Legendary Animal spawning outside the map border
  • Fixed throwing rocks not damaging fish
  • Resolved co-op trapping master skill bugs

Bug fixes

  • Legendary Animal spawning beyond the Valley map border
  • Throwing Rocks not dealing damage to fish
  • Bandit damage multiplier having no effect on bandit weapons in certain cases
  • Trapping Master skill bugs appearing if players were on horses in Co-op mode
