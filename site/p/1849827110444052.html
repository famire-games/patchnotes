Counter-Strike 2 Update

This update fixes various terrains and bugs on the map 'Cobblestone', improves 3rd-person silent reload animation speeds, and enhances overall stability.

Key changes

  • Fixed unreachable locations caused by throwing the bomb at the Princess Tower on Cobblestone
  • Fixed incorrect wallbang locations
  • Fixed general level geometry for smoother player movement
  • Fixed incorrect boosts and climbable ledges
  • Fixed locations where grenades could fall out of bounds in site B
  • Adjusted 3rd-person silent reload animation playback speed

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where the bomb could be thrown into unreachable locations at the Princess Tower
  • Fixed an issue where grenades fell out of the map in site B
  • Fixed various gaps across the map
  • Fixed 3rd-person silent reload animation speed
  • Performed various stability improvements
