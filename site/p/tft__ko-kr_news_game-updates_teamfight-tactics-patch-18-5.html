Teamfight Tactics Patch 18.5

Teamfight Tactics patch 18.5 is a follow-up to patch 18.4, focusing on fine-tuning and buffing underperforming champions while balancing overperforming compositions. Detailed changes have been applied to traits, champions, augments, and guardian spirit systems.

Key changes

  • Fine-tuned and buffed underperforming ability power units and champions.
  • Adjusted trait balances for Black Thorns, Dryad, Fae, Deadly Blossom, Rapidfire, and Canyon Beast.
  • Nerfed overperforming elements in the meta such as Kha'Zix, Bramble Spirit, and Hand of Justice.
  • Adjusted appearance probability weighting based on attack damage and ability power preferences when selecting augments.
  • Adjusted guardian spirit filtering, pricing, and performance balance.

Buffs

  • Caitlyn enhanced basic attack damage increased
  • Broodmother mana lock duration shortened and slightly buffed
  • Master Yi AP form and starting attack damage increased
  • Tristana mana adjusted and skill damage increased
  • Malphite shield absorption increased
  • Morgana damage increased
  • Sett AP-based health restoration increased
  • Sivir skill damage increased
  • Soraka star damage increased
  • Ashe damage over time increased
  • Taric armor, magic resistance, and skill damage increased

Nerfs

  • Kha'Zix base and isolation damage decreased
  • Bramble Spirit armor ignore and health restoration decreased
  • Hand of Justice omnivamp decreased
  • Black Thorns (6) and base AP sacrifice decreased
  • Dryad (9) bonus health decreased
  • Deadly Blossom health restoration decreased
  • Rapidfire attack speed decreased
  • Canyon Beast (7) attack damage and ability power decreased
  • Azir skill damage decreased
  • Warden stun duration decreased and shield absorption adjusted
  • Alune tuning durability decreased

Bug fixes

  • Fixed the Dryad emblem stats to apply correctly.
  • Fixed the Broodmother mana lock bug.
  • Fixed a bug where Electric Charge II augment did not apply damage correctly.
  • Fixed a bug where the Debt of Life guardian spirit triggered incorrectly under lost health conditions.
  • Fixed a bug where in-game chat would automatically send upon reaching a certain length.
