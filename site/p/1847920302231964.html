Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 Season 1 Update

Season 1 of Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 and Warzone launches on November 6. Massive new content is added, including new multiplayer maps and modes, the X01 Black Site in DMZ, and new maps Hajin and Zodiac in Warzone.

Key changes

  • Added multiplayer maps Backstab, Canyon, and Shoot House alongside the start of Season 1
  • Introduced new and returning multiplayer modes such as Grind, Drop Zone, and Gunfight 1v1
  • Added a new region X01 Black Site along with new missions and enemies to DMZ
  • Added new maps Hajin and Zodiac to Warzone and integrated Modern Warfare 4
  • New weapons such as the CSG-35 SMG and Kronen Osprey assault rifle scheduled to arrive mid-season
  • Additional maps and modes such as Ministry and Overflow to be unveiled during Season 1 Reloaded (mid-season)
