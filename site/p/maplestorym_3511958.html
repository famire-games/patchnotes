7/30 Patch: New Character Lyn, Dowarden Region, Combat Power Renewal, and Major Boss Improvements

A new warrior character 'Lyn' and the new Grandis region 'Dowarden' have been added to MapleStory M. The combat power formula has been changed, a core score system has been introduced, and convenience improvements and difficulty adjustments for major boss content such as Lotus, Damien, and Lucid have been implemented.

Key changes

  • Added new Anima warrior character 'Lyn' and related My Home theme/furniture shop
  • Added new Grandis region 'Dowarden' (accessible at Lv. 280 and above) and Authentic Symbols
  • Revamped combat power formula and introduced V/HEXA/Stellar Code core score systems
  • Integrated boss single/multi modes and applied HP systems proportional to party size
  • Added boss attack range indicators and boss phase cutscene skip features
  • Significantly adjusted patterns and difficulty of major bosses including Lotus, Damien, Lucid, Will, and Jin Hilla
