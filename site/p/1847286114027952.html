Season 21 Community Weekend Event Guide

The Season 21 Community Weekend event in Sea of Thieves kicks off on October 23. Various rewards and events await, including Twitch Drops, Community Emissary Grade boosts, and Pop-up Plunder.

Key changes

  • Trickster and Trickster-themed events return to the Quest Board.
  • Community Emissary Grade increases up to Grade 5 based on Twitch watch time.
  • Log in to receive the Season 21 Community Weekend flag and free daily voyages.
  • Pop-up Plunder and Pop-up Blunder events where rewards are found via social channel clues.
  • Photo walls installed at Outposts and Reaper's Hideout.

Buffs

  • Gain 2.5x Gold, Reputation, Season Renown, Allegiance, and Guild Reputation upon reaching Community Emissary Grade 5
