Railroads Online - Kinetic Update Patch 1

Patch 1 for the Kinetic Update of Railroads Online is now live. This update introduces railroad ties to identify industry connection points, adjusts steam consumption and cutoff simulation, adds a tractive effort boost to cylinder valves, and fixes various bugs.

Key changes

  • Connection points at industries are now represented by a railroad tie for easier identification.
  • Steam consumption and cutoff simulation have been adjusted to reasonable levels.
  • Activating cylinder valves now adds a small boost in tractive effort.
  • Proper track linking is now crucial for the new movement system.

Buffs

  • Activating cylinder valves adds a small boost in tractive effort.

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a Fatal Error where Client cars were not properly updated on large save games.
  • Fixed an issue where industries lacked collision or did not accept freight items.
  • Fixed an issue where exhaust particles disappeared when the smokestack was out of the player's view.
  • Fixed an issue where the client water level was not synchronized in the loco UI.
  • Fixed an issue where the handcar was using the firewood storage.
