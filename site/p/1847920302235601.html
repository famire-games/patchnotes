Escape from Tarkov Patch 1.3.0.0

Patch 1.3.0.0 introduces a new Halloween event, fall weather, high-difficulty PMC bots in the PvE Zone, and new Hideout customization options. It also adjusts skill leveling mechanics and fixes various bugs.

Key changes

  • Added a new Halloween in-game event
  • Switched the in-game weather season to fall
  • Added high-difficulty PMC bots to the PvE Zone
  • Added new Hideout customization sets
  • Unity 6 switch moved to mid-November (update 1.3.5.0)

Buffs

  • Significantly increased the leveling speed of all skills in Seasonal mode
  • Increased the lifetime of player-placed tripwires
  • Increased weapon skill leveling speed to match other skills

Bug fixes

  • Fixed incorrect Partisan spawn chance calculation in raids without low PMC karma players
  • Fixed issues with loading/unloading magazines after a weapon malfunction
  • Fixed incorrect field of view for certain optics
  • Fixed access issues to traders' interactive scenes
  • Fixed PMC bot behavior so they always consider players hostile
