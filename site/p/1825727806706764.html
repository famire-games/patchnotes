[No Way Out] Major Update Announcement

UTC 2026-02-26 07:30 ~ 11:30

A major update for Mecarashi titled [No Way Out] is here. It introduces new pilots, STs, weapons, backpacks, stage expansions, and multiple limited-time events.

Key changes

  • Limited-time new pilots and STs (Cassha, Camilla, Gumusiay, Arthur, Erisa)
  • New S+ and SS weapons added (Mountains - B II, Blaze Howl - B, Sivri, Kagutsuchi)
  • New SS Backpacks (Custodian, Conqueror) and Blueprints added
  • Main Story expanded to Chapter 13 and Elite Battles to Chapter 12
  • New events: Milkhama Barrage, Thunder & Lightning, Explorer's Quest Season 6
