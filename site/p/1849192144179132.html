Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord v1.5.7 / War Sails v1.3.7 Patch Notes

This major update expands naval gameplay with ferries, mooring, naval random events, and enhanced boarding mechanics. It also introduces Advanced Starting Options for sandboxes, meaningful personality traits, blood feuds, and granular clan party controls.

Key changes

  • Added a ferry system for coastal travel without ships and port mooring for fleets
  • Introduced Advanced Starting Options (ASO) to customize campaign scenarios, player starts, and modifiers
  • Personality traits now grant distinct positive and negative gameplay effects
  • Executions can now trigger blood feuds between clans that last until resolved
  • Naval boarding ropes can now be targeted and destroyed to prevent enemy bridging
  • Added investigable Battle Sites and Wreckages on the campaign map for loot and experience

Buffs

  • Increased Boatswain skill experience gained from repairing ships

Nerfs

  • Reduced most non-governor and non-naval perks' effects by 50% in naval battles

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug causing lost Seaborne Village Raid simulations to count as wins
  • Fixed party position errors after setting sail that prevented entering settlements
  • Fixed campaign map and mission scenes having inconsistent weather conditions
  • Fixed ship collisions causing excessive camera shakes or multiple damage instances
  • Fixed retreated ships displaying as destroyed in mission scoreboards
