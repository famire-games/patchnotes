VALORANT Patch Notes 13.08

VALORANT Patch 13.08 introduces the competitive 'Map Rating' system to measure map proficiency, alongside updates to performance scores. Additionally, Corrode has been reworked, Bind and Corrode have been added to the map rotation, while Ascent and Split have been removed.

Key changes

  • Introduced the 'Map Rating' system consisting of 0-500 points and C-S tiers for Competitive and Premier
  • Increased influence of damage and K/D adjustments (including first kills/deaths) in performance score calculations, and factored in Chamber's utility
  • Reworked Corrode map structure (defender paths, mid walls and box locations, Site A spike plant zone)
  • Added Bind and Corrode to Competitive and Deathmatch map pools, removed Ascent and Split
  • Revamped matchmaking and custom game interfaces, and added a match history search bar
  • Shared security and anti-cheat status, including public sanction metrics

Buffs

  • Adjusted spawn paths so defenders can move more safely and quickly to Site A and Site B
  • Expanded the mid area and Site A connector pathway for attackers to utilize
  • Adjusted Chamber's utility usage to be better reflected in his score

Nerfs

  • Adjusted the Site A spike plant zone to lower the effectiveness of post-plant holding strategies from the A connector

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug where the Competitive 'Deeds' reward criteria was displayed incorrectly
  • Fixed a bug where character footsteps in the fog of war would occasionally fail to play
  • Fixed a bug where death sound effects on the floor in the fog of war did not play at the correct location
  • Fixed a bug where weapon drop sound effects occasionally failed to trigger properly
  • Fixed a bug where Warden's scope crosshair would misalign during fully automatic fire
