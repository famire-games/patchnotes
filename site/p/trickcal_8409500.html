November 5 Update Notice

Through the regular maintenance on November 5, various updates will take place, including the weekday farm revamp, the start of the Winner's Spoiled Club regular season, and the addition of new Apostles and Assides.

  • Changed the Apostle in the Apostle Interview system to 'Dudu' and held a new pickup
  • Added 4 new production lab body pillows and new Assides
  • Added farm shop categories and sold new items
  • Fixed bugs such as Sylvia's high-grade skill

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where the crepe interaction voice did not play in the November attendance book

  • Fixed an issue where the theme theater [A Perfect Cup!] was not displayed
