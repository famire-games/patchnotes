3.2.1 Bug Fixes (November 20)

The November 20 patch for Once Human reduces the Dissonant Dust gain penalty after the weekly cap, adjusts reward limits for The Echo War, and increases the Resonance Box drop rate alongside various bug fixes.

Key changes

  • Reduced Dissonant Dust penalty after weekly cap from 95% to 60%
  • Adjusted The Echo War weekly boss dungeon reward limit to 80 (Basic and Hard share limit, resets Monday 6:00 AM)
  • Increased Resonance Box drop rate
  • Fixed issue where boss The Echo could skip its damage reduction phase

Buffs

  • Reduced Dissonant Dust penalty from 95% to 60%
  • Increased Resonance Box drop rate

Bug fixes

  • Fixed missing explanation of The Echo War limit rules on the Vision page
  • Fixed issue where players couldn't interact with the Echo Bell in Resonant Movements
  • Fixed background music not stopping after Isles of Abyss Vision dungeons ended
  • Fixed incorrect display of female Shadowlight Prayer Bottoms in certain situations
  • Fixed issue where some languages couldn't be switched properly when typing in chat
