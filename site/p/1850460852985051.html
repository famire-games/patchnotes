Identity Trait Keywords and E.G.O Synergy Renewal Notice

Identity trait keywords are being subdivided and redefined into categories such as job, affiliation, combat means, and basic keywords. In conjunction with E.G.O Threadcaste 5 and new E.G.O releases, trait-keyword-based synergy effects are being added. Additionally, Schema Craft UI/UX improvements, boss selection information displays, and the first survey are scheduled.

Key changes

  • Subdivision and redefinition of trait keywords for all Identities (Job/Status, Affiliation, Combat Means, Basic Keywords, etc.)
  • Basic keywords (Burn, Bleed, etc.) will also be displayed as trait keywords
  • Added trait-keyword-based synergy effects tied to E.G.O Threadcaste 5 and new E.G.O
  • Upcoming synergy effects and Threadcaste 5 additions for certain existing E.G.O (Lifetime Stew, Soda, Blind Obsession, etc.)
  • Improved Schema Craft UI/UX and added difficulty and resistance information to the boss selection screen
  • First Schema Craft survey starting December 3rd at 19:00 KST

Bug fixes

  • Ongoing review and action regarding reported crashes and freezes
