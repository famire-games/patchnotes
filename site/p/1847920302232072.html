The Custom Materials Update Is Here

House Flipper 2 has released the Custom Materials Update, bringing the first wave of expanded community-made custom content support. Players can now create and use custom wallpapers, patterns, and textures across modes, with automatic downloading and syncing for co-op and Mod.io files. Additionally, the update features rendering optimizations, a new in-game content library, and the ability to disable the day/night cycle.

Key changes

  • Added ability to create and use Custom Materials in Sandbox and Story modes
  • Mod.io houses and jobs now automatically list and download missing Custom Assets
  • Automatic synchronization of Custom Materials and Custom Pictures in Co-op
  • Refreshed in-game Mod.io browser and new in-game Content Library
  • Blender Addon and web uploader introduced for custom material creation
  • Added option to disable the Day/Night cycle and improved rendering pipeline performance
