Valheim Patch 1.0.30 Released

Applied major improvements related to the Forge of Potential along with various bug fixes and optimizations. This patch affects crossplay compatibility, requiring all players in multiplayer to update their games.

Key changes

  • The Forge of Potential now returns significantly more materials upon failure.
  • Fixed an issue where initial RNG for the Forge of Potential was affected by character names.
  • Kriogen and Hexen in the Deep North region now give up and stop chasing players indefinitely after a certain time.
  • Catching a 5-star fish unlocks the 'A catch worthy of Njord' achievement.
  • Fixed a character clipping bug after using portals in multiplayer.
  • Added a new accessibility setting to increase brightness in dark environments.

Buffs

  • Substantially increased material refunds upon Forge of Potential failures
  • Achievements now unlock for catching 5-star fish in addition to 4-star fish
  • Headband and Crown of Roots can now be repaired

Nerfs

  • Crossbows no longer deal chop damage

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug where characters clipped into portals after teleporting in multiplayer
  • Fixed a bug where spawn rates for rare creatures (e.g., Sea Serpents) were unintentionally low
  • Fixed a bug where certain vineberries incorrectly disappeared after reloading
  • Fixed continuous flickering of grass near fires
  • Fixed a bug where Asksvins played footsteps sound effects too frequently while idle
