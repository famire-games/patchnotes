Patch 37.0.2 Notes (Battlegrounds)

Strong tribes like Quilboars and Demons were slightly nerfed, while weaker tribes like Pirates were buffed. Murloc's Skilled Aviator was removed, Elemental's Living Azerite was adjusted, and the Dark Gifts system along with various minions, trinkets, and spells were revamped.

Key changes

  • Removed Skilled Aviator from the minion pool
  • Returned Murloc Holmes to the minion pool
  • Changed Living Azerite spellcast Elemental buff from +4/+3 to +3/+3
  • Adjusted Dark Gift appearance timing and effects (reduced early power, buffed mid-to-late game)
  • Added a rule to guarantee 2 or more minions of the most-held tribe after turn 7
  • Adjusted stats and costs of most minions and trinkets
