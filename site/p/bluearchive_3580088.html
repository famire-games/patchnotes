November 12th (Thu) Seamless Patch Notice

A seamless patch for game improvements and stability will take place on Thursday, November 12th, from 3:00 PM to 3:05 PM. Restarting the game after completion will download the patch, which includes the start of a web-view event and various bug fixes.

Key changes

  • Started web-view event 'Arona's Special Mission Event! Season 2'
  • Fixed an issue where equipment upgrade material icons were missing on the minigame (Millennium Sports Festival / Martial Arts Tournament) formation screen
  • Fixed video playback errors in Main Story Vol. 2, Ch. 1, Ep. 16
  • STEAM client displays Adults-Only rating notice

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where equipment upgrade material icons were not displayed in the minigame (Millennium Sports Festival / Martial Arts Tournament) formation screen
  • Fixed an issue where the video would not play during Main Story Vol. 2, Ch. 1, Ep. 2 'We Become the Flame', Episode 16
