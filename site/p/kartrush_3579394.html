KartRider Rush+ · November 11, 2026

Season 42 Secret Garden Update Guide

KartRider Rush+ has introduced the Season 42 'Secret Garden' theme featuring new karts, characters, tracks, and the Fantasy Garden content. Additionally, the Rush Farm max level has been expanded alongside various system improvements and bug fixes.

Key changes

  • Released Season 42 'Secret Garden' theme and new karts (White Knight Anis, Black Knight Croco, Stealer)
  • Opened season-limited 'Fantasy Garden' content featuring new crop cultivation and stealing mechanics
  • Raised Rush Farm max levels (Farm 175, Ranch 62, Restaurant 77, Fishery 400 Depth)
  • Added new tracks 'Ice Thrilling High-Altitude Jump' and 'Desert Pharaoh's Sanctuary'
  • Started Season 42 Ranked Mode and added new achievement 'Conqueror of the Lake'
  • Returned Ultimate League and optimized Merge Town UI

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where special effects within Galaxy borders were not displayed
  • Unified the names of Siren Booster and Super Siren to Super Siren
  • Changed Parrot pet skill effect description (added self-destruction net bomb defense)
  • Fixed an issue where Lucci acquired on tracks when using Lucci Booster skills was not paid out upon settlement
