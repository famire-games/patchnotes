Snowbreak: Containment Zone · July 8, 2025

Concord Ode Update

The latter half of main story Chapter 15 and the 'Concord Ode' limited event have been added. New pickups for Katya and Tess, along with various mini-games and challenge contents, are now available.

Key changes

  • Added main story Chapter 15 Pt.2 [Concord Ode] with quick access feature
  • Opened event shop [Acropolis Agora] to exchange various rewards
  • Added mini-games such as [Garden Mayhem], [Bluff or Blush], and [Goldrush Party]
  • Added [Builder Island] building and management content
  • Held limited operator Echo pickups for Katya and Tess
  • Opened various challenge content including [Supreme Battleground] and [Containment Protocol]
