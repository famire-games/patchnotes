WWElias Regular Season Open

The regular season of Trickcal Re:Dive's weekend content, WWElias, has begun. High Priests can predict and cheer for winning match teams to earn rewards. The content unlocks upon clearing Mild 4-10.

Key changes

  • Weekend content WWElias regular season runs (11/6 - 11/8)
  • Unlocks upon clearing Mild 4-10
  • Predict match outcomes and check results across a total of 4 rounds
  • Provides Sion's match information briefing feature
  • Earn additional rewards based on successful cheer counts
  • Replay past match results after the event concludes
