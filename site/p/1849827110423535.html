Anno 117: Pax Romana – Patch 3.0.1

Patch 3.0.1 expands coastal building space in northern Aegyptus and resolves several gameplay exploits, including Granary buff stacking. Major stability and performance issues, such as console memory crashes and large settlement frame drops, have also been addressed. In addition, extensive bug fixes were applied to specialists, diplomacy, and the UI.

Key changes

  • Additional coastal space added to three northern islands in Aegyptus for harbour buildings
  • Granary buffs no longer stack, applying only the highest-level buff in the area
  • Fixed Heron's Wax Tablet trade exploit in Aegyptus, replacing it with Scribe Sets
  • Rebalanced legendary specialist Tantiemani to apply only to Marvels
  • Major performance improvements addressing console crashes, memory leaks, and stuttering

Buffs

  • Three islands in northern Aegyptus received additional coastal space for harbour buildings

Nerfs

  • Granary buffs no longer stack; only the buff from the highest-level Granary in the area is applied
  • Legendary specialist Tantiemani's effect now only applies to Marvels instead of all public service buildings in Aegyptus
  • Heron in Aegyptus no longer sells Wax Tablets while rebuying them at a higher price (now requests Scribe Sets)

Bug fixes

  • Fixed severe frame rate drops on PS5 in large settlements and out-of-memory console crashes
  • Fixed pedestrian spawn limits not functioning, which caused excessive memory usage and crashes
  • Fixed Rivals not offering higher-tier goods for trade and suffering stalled economies
  • Fixed crashes on Intel Arc GPUs occurring in the main menu or while loading saves
  • Fixed trade route waypoint pins being impossible to move or focus
