Hearts of Iron IV · November 18, 2026

To the Bitter End Patch Notes

Alongside the new focus pack, this update re-balances aspects of the Eastern Front, improves various nation AIs, and fixes several bugs in Hearts of Iron IV.

Key changes

  • Adjusted focuses and national spirits for Finland, France, and the Soviet Union
  • Added land forts representing the Stalin Line
  • Reworked Romanian MIOs, tech icons, leaders, and added new general/admiral
  • Improved AI behavior for Germany, Soviet Union, UK, Japan, and Finland

Buffs

  • FIN: Sissi and National Armor Focus now grant a 20% mastery bonus for 180 days
  • FRA: France starts with Stronghold Network unlocked in 1936
  • SOV: Desperate Measures provides +50% mobilization speed and -10% minimum training level

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug where division_has_battalion_in_template only checked the last battalion or support company
  • Research window tab tooltips now state correct shortcuts (Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab)
  • Fixed a bug where Volyn was not assigned to Reichskommissariat Ukraine
