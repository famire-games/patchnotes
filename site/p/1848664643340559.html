Update 12.2.0 Patch Notes

This update features store rotations, information on the TGM26 cosplay showcase, and balance adjustments for Medium class automatic and semi-automatic weapons. The focus is on improving the Medium's combat capability against the 175-health Light class.

Key changes

  • Added new store items (Goat Expectations set and Dual Shockers set)
  • Information on the TGM26 cosplay showcase at DreamHack Stockholm
  • Adjusted Medium class weapon balances (AKM, Chimera-XB, FCAR)
  • Buffs and adjustments aimed at improving matchup balance between Light and Medium classes

Buffs

  • AKM: Magazine capacity increased from 34 to 36
  • Chimera-XB: Damage increased from 47 to 48, fire rate increased from 260 RPM to 280 RPM
  • FCAR: Damage increased from 23 to 25

Nerfs

  • FCAR: Fire rate decreased from 530 RPM to 500 RPM, magazine capacity decreased from 25 to 20
