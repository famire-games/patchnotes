10.3.0 Terrifier Patch Notes

The new Killer 'Terrifier', Survivor 'Sienna Shaw', and a new map 'Hearththorn Mall' have been added. Adjustments to existing perks, the addition of gore settings, and a rework of the Mori system have been implemented.

Key changes

  • Added new Killer 'Terrifier' and unique power 'Toy Bag'
  • Added new Survivor 'Sienna Shaw' and 3 new perks
  • Added new map 'Hearththorn Mall'
  • Adjusted multiple Survivor and Killer perks
  • Introduced custom gore settings and a reworked Mori system

Buffs

  • Dark Sense: Added a token system allowing activation when the Killer approaches, in addition to completing generators
  • Calm Spirit: Token and chest rummage speeds increased to 23/24/25%
  • Slippery Meat: Unhook speed bonus increased to 7%
  • Whispers: Movement speed increased by 7% when outside a certain distance from survivors

Nerfs

  • Slippery Meat: Reduced unhook speed bonus granted to others
  • Hubris: Vault speed bonus locked and reduced to 15%
  • Starstruck: Exposed duration upon reaching 4 tokens decreased to 60/65/70 seconds

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where Sienna Shaw's lobby voice lines were audible to the Killer
  • Fixed missing escape gate opening voice lines for Sienna Shaw
  • Fixed a footstep audio sync issue during Terrifier's toy charge
  • Fixed missing Mori finisher sound effects for Terrifier against female survivors
  • Removed the rummage limit on chests
