7/30 All-Class Balance Revision

A balance revision for all classes was carried out in MapleStory M, standardizing base stats, adjusting skill stats and damage, and revamping the balance of certain classes. Notably, this included resolving imbalances between classes and adjusting party support and invincibility effects.

Key changes

  • Standardized base stats for all classes (attack power, defense, HP, MP, critical rate, etc.)
  • Completely adjusted skill stats and skill damage for all classes
  • Revamped balance for certain classes and adjusted party support and invincibility
  • Improved Hyper Passive skill point acquisition (maximum 9 points acquired in units of 5 levels)
  • Corrected miswritten patch notes (deleted some content related to Battle Mage and Illium)

Buffs

  • Increased DPS and improved usability for certain classes

Nerfs

  • Adjusted party support abilities and party invincibility effects for certain classes

Bug fixes

  • Reflected deletion of changes to Battle Mage Shelter invincibility buff duration
  • Reflected deletion of changes to Illium Magic Gauntlet mastery status ailment resistance
