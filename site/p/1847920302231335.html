10.3.0 PTB Patch Notes

The Dead by Daylight 10.3.0 PTB patch introduces the new Killer 'The Terrafier', new Survivor 'Cian Shaw', and new map 'Harthshorn Mall'. It also applies gore customization features and a Mori system overhaul to gameplay settings, along with adjustments to several survivor and killer perks and various bug fixes.

Key changes

  • Added new Killer 'The Terrafier' and 3 unique perks
  • Added new Survivor 'Cian Shaw' and 3 unique perks
  • Added new map 'Harthshorn Mall' and the Harts Subdivision realm
  • Added gore customization (adjusting blood stain colors, execution visual effects, etc.) within gameplay settings
  • Overhauled the Mori system (classified into Finishers, Powers, Memento Moris/Perk Moris)
  • Adjusted numerous survivor and killer perks (Dark Sense, Iron Will, Dark Arrogance, etc.)

Buffs

  • Dark Sense: Added a token system allowing tokens to be earned and utilized outside of generator completions
  • Calm Spirit: Increased token and crow cleansing, and chest unlocking speed to 23/24/25%
  • Slippery Meat: Increased the Haste movement speed effect after unhooking to 7%
  • Whispers: Increased the Haste movement speed effect gained when outside 28/26/24m of a survivor to 7%

Nerfs

  • Dark Arrogance: Base vault speed bonus reduced from 25% to 15%, and added a basic attack miss cooldown penalty (15% increase) alongside blindness and pallet stuns
  • Laimus: Reduced the duration of the 'Exposed' status effect applied to survivors upon reaching 4 tokens to 60/65/70 seconds
  • Slippery Meat: Reduced the speed bonus for other survivors unhooking you to 65/70/75%

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where survivor bots would try to cleanse tokens while a player was interacting with them
  • Fixed an issue where completed generator auras were visible during Endgame Collapse after controlling Victor
  • Fixed an issue on Raccoon City Police Department where killers could block hooks to prevent rescues
  • Fixed an issue where the generator regression speed increase from the perk 'Wreck's Waste' was applied before other generator regression perks
  • Fixed an issue when facing The Doctor with the 'Merciless Storm' perk where skill checks would automatically fail after the second skill check
