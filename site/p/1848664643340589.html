Anno 117: Pax Romana – Update 3.0

Update 3.0 and the 'Dawn of the Delta' DLC for Anno 117: Pax Romana are now live. This update brings the new Stamp Mode, expanded Statistics Screen tabs, various balancing changes, and extensive bug fixes.

Key changes

  • Added Stamp Mode to save, name, and organize residential or production layouts
  • Added Storage and Item/Specialist tabs to the Statistics Screen
  • Changed default difficulty for new save games to 'Normal', increasing goods consumption
  • Rebalanced festival costs and required goods based on progression
  • Optimized install and update sizes on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S
  • Added option to hide quests from the quest log

Buffs

  • Increased starting money to compensate for the tougher early game
  • Reduced festival costs from mid-game onward (end-game cost cut from 50,000 to 25,000 coins)
  • Increased Latium Mills production speed
  • Slightly increased Aqueduct Cistern range to match the updated area of effect preview

Nerfs

  • Reduced aqueduct-related specialists' water supply bonus (Aquila Dulcis and Ductuarius)
  • Increased maintenance and workforce requirements for Latium Mills
  • Increased goods consumption for new save games

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a crash when clicking the 'Expired Notes' notification
  • Fixed autosave limit not working on consoles
  • Fixed wine-related goods missing from trading posts, trade routes, and statistics
  • Fixed military units unable to pass through destroyed enemy gate ruins
  • Fixed an issue where ships sent to the edge of the map became unselectable with a gamepad
