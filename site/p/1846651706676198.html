Deadlock 10-14-2026 Minor Patch

This patch adjusts stats for heroes and items including Radiant Regeneration, Baba, and Deadman Danny. Baba received overall nerfs, while Deadman Danny received buffs.

Key changes

  • Reduced Radiant Regeneration's Spirit scaling.
  • Nerfed Baba's Spirit resistance and base gun resistance.
  • Nerfed Baba's overall skill cooldowns and shield effects.
  • Increased Deadman Danny's health regen and base health.
  • Buffed Deadman Danny's gun damage and skill power.
  • Reduced Deadman Danny's rocket jump self-damage and changed it to Spirit damage.

Buffs

  • Deadman Danny: Increased health regeneration (2.5 -> 3.25)
  • Deadman Danny: Increased base health (680 -> 730)
  • Deadman Danny: Increased reserve gun base bullet damage (12 -> 15)
  • Deadman Danny: Increased charged gun bullet damage (44 -> 48)
  • Deadman Danny: Reduced Strix Salvo cooldown (30s -> 25s)
  • Deadman Danny: Reduced rocket jump self-damage and changed to Spirit damage

Nerfs

  • Radiant Regeneration: Reduced Spirit scaling (0.04 -> 0.035)
  • Baba: Reduced Spirit resistance scaling (0.12 -> 0.1)
  • Baba: Added -7% base gun resistance
  • Baba: Threadsap - Enemy Spirit reduction effect ends upon Baba's death
  • Baba: Increased Threadsap cooldown (26s -> 28s)
  • Baba: Increased Granny Long Legs cooldown (30s -> 32s)
  • Baba: Reduced Baba's Brew shield scaling (1.2 -> 0.7)
  • Baba: Reduced Baba's Brew shield amount (100 -> 90)
  • Baba: Increased Baba's Brew cooldown (25s -> 27s)
