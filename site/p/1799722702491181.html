Baldur's Gate 3 · May 14, 2025

Baldur's Gate 3 Hotfix #31 Release

This hotfix resolves various bugs such as screen color corruption when moving between regions and inventory-related crashes. Furthermore, balance adjustments related to new subclasses and gameplay errors have been improved.

Key changes

  • Fixed graphic corruption issues occurring when changing regions
  • Fixed crashes occurring when hovering over specific items during inventory management and trading
  • Class balance adjustments including adding a level 2 spell swapping feature for Hexblade Warlocks
  • Increased throw damage for Barbarians while enraged
  • Fixed file system errors for non-Latin character accounts related to Photo Mode
  • Fixed skill tooltip display errors

Buffs

  • Hexblade Warlocks can now swap spells at level 2 like other Warlocks.
  • Increased the damage bonus for thrown objects while raging from +2 to +3 when Barbarians are level 9 or higher.
  • Vaprak's Greed now grants an additional quarter increase to carrying capacity to account for human versatility.

Nerfs

  • Changed Piercing Arrow so that its total damage is halved, affecting both base and bonus damage.

Bug fixes

  • Fixed mod-related crashes during level loading
  • Fixed high-saturation graphic artifacts that occurred during teleportation
  • Fixed an issue where Shadowheart's parents-related choices resulted in her failing to wake up after a long rest if she died outside of camp
  • Fixed a bug where corpses could not be placed inside containers in controller or split-screen UI
