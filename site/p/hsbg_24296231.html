Patch 36.4.2 Notes (Battlegrounds)

Focusing on the highest and lowest-performing minion types in the Battlegrounds meta, this patch buffs Beasts, Demons, Elementals, and Quilboars while nerfing Elementals and Nagas. Additionally, armor values for multiple heroes, minions, Tavern spells, and trinkets have been adjusted.

Key changes

  • Buffs focused on Beasts, Demons, Quilboars, and Dragons
  • Nerfs to Elementals (Storm of Released Magic) and Nagas (Blessing of Nature, etc.)
  • Adjusted armor values for heroes such as Reno Jackson and Inge, Iron Hymn
  • Removed the Tavern spell 'Consecration'
  • Changed Lesser and Greater Trinket pools, and adjusted costs and effects

Buffs

  • Tasty Lobster (Base buff increased to +2/+1)
  • Great Wolf Goldrinn (Moved to Tier 5, stats adjusted)
  • Lurking Behemoth (Attack bonus increased to +3)
  • Buffed Eredar Renegade and self-damage-related Demon minions
  • Buffed Pyreborn Invoker and Mighty Dragonbreath
  • Buffed Quilboar-related cards such as Blood Champion and Blood Refiner

Nerfs

  • Storm of Released Magic (Adjusted Elemental buff amount)
  • Blessing of Nature (Cost 4 -> 2, adjusted buff amount)
  • Secret Splicer (Moved to Tier 4)
  • Decreased armor for heroes such as Reno Jackson and Inge, Iron Hymn
  • Increased costs and strengthened trigger conditions for certain Lesser/Greater Trinkets
