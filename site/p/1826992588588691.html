Rocket League v2.66 Patch Notes

The Rocket League v2.66 update has been applied. Features include the return of playlist player counts, flip reset visual and audio effects, competitive MMR display, large boost pad timer visualizations, and custom training settings.

Key changes

  • Player counts for playlists can now be seen again in the play menu
  • Added visual and audio effects upon successful flip resets (can be disabled in settings)
  • Added an option to display MMR on competitive playlist tiles and the post-match scoreboard
  • Added a visual charge indicator for the 10-second respawn on large boost pads
  • Added various custom training settings such as ball and car speed/position randomization
  • Replaced Item Shop and Garage in the queue menu with Freeplay and Challenges

Bug fixes

  • Removed unintentional wall graffiti that blocked goal visibility in The Block (Hoops) arena
  • Fixed an issue where the cage in the variant version of Beckwith Park arena hindered player vision
  • Fixed an issue preventing custom tournament creation
  • Fixed duplicate messages appearing in the in-match notification feed during demolitions
  • Fixed Gravity Bomb goal explosions destroying cars
