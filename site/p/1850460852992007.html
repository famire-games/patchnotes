November 27 Minor Update

A minor update for Deadlock has been released, featuring balance adjustments for Apollo and Baba. Apollo's Riposte has been buffed, while Baba receives several cooldown reductions and damage increases.

Key changes

  • Apollo's Riposte now procs on melee hits
  • Baba's base bullet resistance increased
  • Baba receives various cooldown reductions and damage buffs

Buffs

  • Apollo: Riposte cooldown reduced and T2 debuff duration increased
  • Baba: Bullet resistance increased, Threadsap cooldown reduced and damage increased
  • Baba: Granny Long Legs cooldown reduced and heavy melee scaling increased
