Overwatch Patch Notes (November 4, 2026)

Including the 'Reign' event starting November 4, the OWCS World Finals support tournament and Junkrat's Junk Hunt are underway. The intentional teammate sabotage detection system has been improved, and a number of hero balance adjustments and Stadium mode updates have been applied.

Key changes

  • 'Reign' event runs from November 4 to December 8
  • Improved intentional teammate sabotage prevention tools and added thank you messages
  • Large-scale balance adjustments for Tank, Damage, and Support heroes
  • Re-applied Stadium competitive group composition restrictions
  • Changed major perks and ability values for each hero

Buffs

  • Increased Junker Queen base health and reduced Rampage cooldown
  • Increased Mauga base armor and Incendiary Chainguns persistent burn damage
  • Increased Ramattra Void Accelerator ammo count
  • Increased Anlan's Zhuque Fan attack power and reduced Zhuque Ascendant cast time
  • Added ammo replenishment when Echo uses aerial ammo
  • Reduced Emre Cyber Shrapnel grenade explosion time
  • Increased Reaper base health
  • Reduced Sion execution initiation scatter radius and reduced Frenzied Slaughter firing/movement time per burst
  • Reduced Brigitte Inspire internal cooldown and increased instant healing amount
  • Reduced Illari Solar Rifle maximum charge time and increased Healing Sunstone healing amount
  • Reduced Jetpack Cat's Frazzled Flight acceleration

Nerfs

  • Reduced D.Mon Extra Punch life steal, increased Power Matrix recovery time, and removed Boosters impact damage
  • Reduced Winston Primal Wellspring barrier healing per second
  • Reduced Anlan's Fierce Flame Dash knockback effect and multi-hit capability
  • Reduced Genji Deflect duration
  • Reduced Vendetta Palatine Fang slamming leap speed and initial swing delay, and reduced Vortex Dash knockback intensity
  • Reduced Ana Biotic Grenade explosion damage
  • Increased Doctrine Aeon Scepter recovery time, and increased Infusion and Propelling Barrier cooldowns
  • Reduced Jetpack Cat base health and fuel regeneration rate
  • Reduced Sombra Emergency Patch duration and Cyberspace debuff effect

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a weapon audio issue for Sierra's Made My Night skin
  • Fixed an issue where D.Mon's speed was abnormally fast when using Aerial Boosters
  • Fixed an issue where Mizuki got stuck on the payload in Circuit Royal
  • Fixed an issue where Wrecking Ball's rolling movement speed increased significantly when the Grappling Claw was attached
  • Fixed getting stuck and line-of-sight issues in Eichenwalde and Watchpoint: Gibraltar
