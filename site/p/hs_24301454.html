Patch Notes 37.0

Releasing on October 14, Patch 37.0 kicks off the pre-release Tavern Brawl for the new expansion 'Dominion of the Black Empire', updates the Arena season, introduces the Tavern Rewards system, and launches various in-game events. Furthermore, achievements have been renamed to 'Feats of Strength' and various bugs have been fixed.

Key changes

  • New expansion 'Dominion of the Black Empire' launches on October 21 with a pre-release Tavern Brawl
  • Introduced 'Tavern Rewards' to grant extra bonuses upon Battle.net Shop purchases
  • Introduced the 'Journey' journal system featuring stories, quests, and rewards
  • Diablo crossover '30 Years of Terror' event and Tavern Brawl
  • Achievements renamed to 'Feats of Strength'
  • New Arena season starts with the Ilgynoth hero skin rewarded for 12 wins

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug where Infinite Flame always triggered first at the end of the opponent's turn
  • Fixed a bug where Shadow of Demise did not interact correctly with Cult Neophyte
  • Fixed a bug where Beaststalker Tavish could Discover Colossal minions
  • Fixed a bug where Spiked Mount did not grant Taunt to the targeted minion
  • Fixed a bug where Professor Slate hero skin was missing a combat voice line
