Honkai: Star Rail · April 21, 2026

Version 4.2 "So, the Laughter Shall Never Stop" Update

The Honkai: Star Rail Version 4.2 update brings the new Trailblaze Mission "Ideal Paradise" and new characters Silver Wolf LV.999, Evanesia, and Trailblazer (Elation). Additionally, content and system optimizations such as Currency War season expansion and Express Modification Encounters have been added.

Key changes

  • Added new Trailblaze Mission "Ideal Paradise" - "So, the Laughter Shall Never Stop"
  • Added new 5-star characters Silver Wolf LV.999, 5-star Evanesia, and 5-star Trailblazer (Elation)
  • Added new fields Ceefield City and Ceefield TV Tower
  • Expanded the "Currency War • Zero-Sum Game" season and integrated cycle scores
  • Added Express Modification Encounters (bedroom display/gaming area, multi-party slots, etc.)
  • Added Relic preset set saving feature and automatic consumable recovery feature

Bug fixes

  • Fixed abnormal issues related to Ashveil's (The Hunt • Lightning) technique visual effects and debuffs
  • Fixed an issue where Cyrene's (Remembrance • Ice) movement speed did not increase after using her technique
  • Fixed description errors and spread attack target selection errors for the enemy "Semi-Creator, Overflowing Hatred" during the battle [Resist and Perish]
  • Fixed abnormal issues with certain Equations and Curios within Divergent Universe: Paradise Codex
  • Fixed incorrect Trailblazer gender display in character text messages and the inability to acquire books
