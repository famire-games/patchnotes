Manor Lords Beta Patch 0.8.117

This beta update reworks farming growth and fertility cycles while boosting rye yields. It also adjusts balance for foresters, beekeepers, and combat regeneration, alongside addressing multiple crashes and gameplay bugs.

Key changes

  • Farming overhaul: fertility and growth concentrate near cycle end; unplanted fields recover fertility as fallow
  • Rye fertility bonus increased (x1.5 to x1.75) and yield increased by 7%
  • Forester tree planting density reduced by roughly 66%
  • Zeidler (Honey Hunting) affinity and hive establishment mechanics adjusted
  • Fast unit HP regeneration now requires being at least 160m from enemies
  • AI improvements for road layouts, central market enclosures, and building placement

Buffs

  • Rye fertility bonus increased from x1.5 to x1.75 and crop yield increased by 7%
  • Fields with selected crops that remain unplanted now regenerate fertility as if fallow

Nerfs

  • Forester tree planting density reduced by approximately 66%
  • Smoked Fish trade value reduced slightly
  • Weiden Hinterlanders mining boost reduced by 10%
  • Other fertility types replenish slightly less while crops grow, making fallow cycles more necessary
  • Fast HP regeneration requires units to be at least 160 meters away from enemy units

Bug fixes

  • Fixed Zeidler Honey Hunting chance being inverted with woodland coverage
  • Fixed multiple crashes including banner carriers on load, Manor wall combat, and butcher gantry upgrades
  • Fixed raiders stealing Dung and Fertilizer
  • Fixed perks causing double positive or double negative farming modifiers
  • Fixed production limits not functioning for Mines, Quarries, and Gatherer Camps
