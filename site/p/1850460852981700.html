Update 2.61.0.42

Update 2.61.0.42 introduces magnetic torpedo fuses, degaussing coils, and on-the-fly depth charge settings for naval battles. It also rebalances air defenses in Nuclear Escalation, adds depth charges to select aircraft, upgrades DLSS, and fixes numerous bugs.

Key changes

  • Added magnetic signatures to steel-hulled vessels and introduced the Degaussing Coils modification
  • Added in-battle torpedo setting configuration for USS Raleigh, USS Fletcher, Emden, and Z43
  • Added salvo firing and on-the-fly detonation depth adjustment for depth charges on select vessels
  • Added depth charge armaments to the PBY-5A Catalina and He 111 H-16
  • Rebalanced convoy and strongpoint air defense compositions in Nuclear Escalation across multiple BRs
  • Upgraded DLSS Neural Rendering to a newer AI model with improved performance

Buffs

  • Increased depth charge capacity and added an extra depth charge modification for Z43
  • Increased average rewards for damaging and destroying ships in Nuclear Escalation

Nerfs

  • Blocked off unintended elevated positions above the southern team's spawns on American Desert
  • Nuclear Escalation: FlaRakPz 1 at BR 11.3–13.0 now uses Roland 1 instead of Roland 3 missiles

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue causing the torpedo depth selection to disappear from the spawn window
  • Fixed inverted controls in the Bomb/Missile Camera View when using a gamepad controller
  • Fixed SIDAM 25 (Mistral) muzzle flash and overheat effects showing on missile launchers instead of 25 mm cannons
  • Fixed a bug where Puma VJTF missile approach warning icons moved outside the screen bounds
  • Fixed performance drops on Sun City and duplicate stage airfields on Poland
