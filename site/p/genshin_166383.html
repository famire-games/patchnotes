Version 7.1 「Requiem of the Dead」 Update

Genshin Impact Version 7.1 「Requiem of the Dead」 is updated, introducing new characters Vesna and Vodyanitsa, and opening Archon Quest Chapter VII Acts III and IV. Additionally, various content and system optimizations such as the Starry Sea World, Genius Invokation TCG, Imaginarium Theater, and Spiral Abyss have been introduced.

Key changes

  • Added new 5-star characters 「Vesna (Anemo)」 and 「Vodyanitsa (Hydro)」
  • Permanently opened Archon Quest Chapter VII Act III 「The White Night Awakening from a Dream」 and Act IV 「Requiem of the Dead」
  • Added new limited and event weapons (Fable of the Butterfly, Hymn of the Waves, Young Shoot, Frozen Heart, Zephyr Bow, Silver Steel Sword)
  • Overhauled Starry Sea World costume sets and lobby themes, and added 'First Top-Up Bonus'
  • Added new Imaginarium Theater seasons and the third-person shooting mode combat content 「Shadow Trial」 stage
  • Updated Spiral Abyss and Genius Invokation TCG content

Bug fixes

  • Fixed collision boundaries and weapon display errors for certain field objects during Archon Quest progression
  • Fixed enemy AI and animation errors, such as ice coffin visual effects remaining after defeating enemies
  • Fixed an issue where Qiqi's and Beidou's Talents and Constellation effects were intermittently not applied
  • Fixed some errors related to Pure Cryo creation interactions and unlocking
  • Fixed shooting height and level application errors within third-person shooting mode combat content
