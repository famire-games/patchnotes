November 18th Patch Notes

Knight Online has introduced new events such as the Puzzle Event, Full Moon Rift, and Catch the Golden Pig, alongside updates to Event Manager Aset sales. Various system adjustments have been made, including an increased Genie restart cooldown, removed mana consumption in the Discipline Dungeon, and reduced item and Noah drop rates.

Key changes

  • Started Puzzle Event, Full Moon Rift Event, and Catch the Golden Pig Event
  • Started Event Manager Aset sales including potions, pet scrolls, and special Nereids
  • Increased Genie restart cooldown from 10 seconds to 60 seconds
  • Prevented mana consumption when attacking monsters in the Discipline Dungeon
  • Increased selling prices for Low and Middle Class Upgrade Scrolls
  • Reduced item and Noah drop rates

Nerfs

  • Genie restart cooldown increased from 10 seconds to 60 seconds
  • Item and Noah drop rates reduced
