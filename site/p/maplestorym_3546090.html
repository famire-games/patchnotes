September 17th (Thu) Patch Update

The September 17th patch for MapleStory M introduced new systems such as Combat Power Guide, Union Stats, and Freestyle Coupons. Various quality-of-life improvements were also made, including bulk soul crafting, potential resetting, and making Buff Freezers permanent.

Key changes

  • Added Combat Power Guide and revamped Union Stats
  • Added Freestyle Coupons to remove gender restrictions
  • Added bulk soul crafting and potential resetting features
  • Made Buff Freezer effects permanent and organized related items/currencies
  • Permanently opened the Pocket Slot and expanded the maximum single transaction limit in the Meso Market
  • Expanded base and maximum inventory/storage slots by 100 and 60 respectively

Bug fixes

  • Fixed guide text typos in Challengers World Goldrich VIP Membership PLUS
