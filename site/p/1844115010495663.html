Rocket League Season 24 Update

Rocket League Season 24 kicks off on September 23, introducing various new features such as Honor Duel, Black Market Trade-In, and custom scoreboards. Three limited-time events, along with a new Rocket Pass and rewards, are also featured.

Key changes

  • Added Honor Duel, allowing players to request a 1v1 showdown after a match ends
  • Added the ability to trade in 3 duplicate Black Market items for a new item or an uncollected painted version
  • Introduced an MVP-focused podium highlighting the match MVP at the end of games
  • Added a Custom Scoreboard allowing players to hand-pick 4 stats to display on the in-game scoreboard
  • Updated keyboard/mouse controls with a new Freecam and modernized key bindings
  • Granted rank-based exclusive titles to top 100 players on the Soccar Ranked leaderboard
