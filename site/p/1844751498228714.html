Manor Lords Beta Update (0.8.110)

This beta update addresses save corruption and crash issues, overhauls the farming system with mandatory crop rotation, and introduces a new 'Rural Foundations' development perk category. New additions include the Fishmonger artisan, Dunghill building, and a fertilizer production system.

Key changes

  • Fixed save corruption and loading crash issues
  • Overhauled farming with mandatory crop rotation and seasonal crop splits
  • Added 'Rural Foundations' development perk category with 6 new perks
  • Added new Fishmonger artisan and Dunghill building
  • Introduced dung collection and fertilizer production mechanics
  • Improved AI town prosperity and behavior logic

Buffs

  • Warbows damage increased by 23%
  • Cow and Sheep breeding chance increased to 40%
  • Fishing spots increased to 15 per Resource Node

Nerfs

  • Crossbows damage decreased by 16%
  • Fallow Fields regenerate fertility at a significantly lower rate
  • Deep Mine output decreased
  • Fertility boost from Rich Fertility in a Region lowered by 30%

Bug fixes

  • Fixed save corruption and crash on load
  • Fixed oxen logistics jams and pathfinding issues
  • Fixed building speed and oxen-related bugs
  • Fixed picking wrong Origin Perk when having rich Iron and rich Small game
  • Fixed building backyard extensions on plots where no family had moved in
