Hearthstone 37.2 Patch Preview — Battlegrounds Season 15: Twisted Empire

Battlegrounds Season 15 begins with the 37.2 patch on December 1st. Three new keywords and two new heroes are added, the Timewarp Tavern returns, and Trinkets are rotated out. A limited-time 2v2 Tavern Brawl will also open. (Based on the official preview; final content is subject to change)

Key changes

  • Battlegrounds Season 15 'Twisted Empire' begins December 1st, Battlegrounds ratings reset
  • New keywords: Sturdy (takes half damage when attacked), Quest (grants a one-time reward upon completion), Elemental Origin (Tavern Spells that synergize well with Elementals)
  • New heroes: Eternus, Boss Misha (Hero Power 'Sharpened Strategy')
  • Timewarp Tavern returns: Purchase special cards with Chronum on turns 6 and 9
  • Trinkets rotated out, Stealth keyword removed from the bonus pool
  • Limited-time 2v2 Tavern Brawl
