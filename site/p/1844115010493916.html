September 17 Update Notice

The new season '26SP5 Seasonless Garden' has begun, featuring character balance adjustments for Mara, Michelle, Yugiri, and Flavia, along with the new weapon 'Whisperwind'. Additionally, a new 'Assault' role has been introduced alongside an overhauled event center and various new events.

Key changes

  • Added the new 'Assault' role (changes to Ming, Mara, Ciel, and Flavia)
  • Added the new universal primary weapon 'Whisperwind' (automatic crossbow)
  • Introduced a weapon pool system allowing players to choose between Superstring-exclusive weapons and universal primary weapons
  • Kicked off the 26SP5 'Seasonless Garden' season and added new Warrant rewards
  • Adjusted balance for key characters including Mara, Michelle, Yugiri, Flavia, and Leona
  • Overhauled the Event Center UI and hosted a new collaboration event

Buffs

  • Mara: Increased primary weapon Night Sight effective range and base damage buff
  • Michelle: Added armor restoration effect to passive skill and reduced active skill cooldown
  • Yugiri: Buffed primary weapon and skill balance, increased Toxic Mist duration and range
  • Flavia: Increased primary weapon firing rate and magazine capacity, increased active skill duration

Nerfs

  • Mara: Decreased primary weapon base fire rate
  • Yugiri: Slightly decreased primary weapon base damage
  • Flavia: Decreased primary weapon base damage
  • Nora: Decreased active skill missile damage
  • Ming: Changed passive skill's bonus armor damage to apply only to summons

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where enemies continued to take damage after Yugiri left Toxic Mist
  • Fixed an issue where enemies entering Toxic Mist after its creation were unaffected
