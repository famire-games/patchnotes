War Thunder Community Update and 2027 Plans

This War Thunder update introduces GNSS-guided cluster bombs, a 3rd person aiming zone indicator for helicopters, and coastal missile systems, alongside major improvements to ground vehicle ammo loading logic. New vehicles such as the VBCI Philoctetes, VN20, T-80BV-RM, and Mirage 2000-9 are also previewed.

Key changes

  • Introduction of GNSS-guided cluster weapons like the AGM-154A JSOW
  • 3rd person aiming zone indicator for helicopters
  • Coastal missile systems added to Nuclear Escalation
  • Improved ammo loading logic with recommended default loads and hangar ammo slider
  • New vehicle previews: VBCI Philoctetes, VN20, T-80BV-RM, and Mirage 2000-9
  • Free decal code 'SEKRITNIGEL' available until December 18th
