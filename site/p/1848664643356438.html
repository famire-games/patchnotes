Battlefield 6 Game Update 2.1.2.0

Battlefield 6 Update 2.1.2.0 introduces the European Casino map, Casino Heist event, seasonal Leaderboards, and extensive weapon and attachment balancing. It also brings the M142 MRAS to Conquest, new modes to Vegas Strip, and numerous quality-of-life improvements.

Key changes

  • New Map: European Casino
  • New Event: Casino Heist with limited-time DOUGH currency
  • New seasonal Leaderboards system with career, mode, and class filtering
  • New additions: NSW4 Carbine, ADS-17 6GHz gadget, and M142 MRAS vehicle
  • Expanded Vegas Strip to support Rush, Team Deathmatch, and Domination
  • Server Browser Limited Public Scale Test rolling out

Buffs

  • GRT-BC base aim-down-sights speed increased by one tier
  • M/60 with 50-Round Loose Belt now grants 250 total rounds on spawn instead of 150

Nerfs

  • Mini Scout: Added a third damage drop-off step at 150m, removing one-hit headshot kills beyond 150m
  • SCW-10: Added damage drop-off steps at 55m and 75m
  • KTS100 MK8: Adjusted magazine attributes and handling to prevent AR-like behavior
  • Extended Magazines (KV9, SCW-10, SG 553R): Added draw speed and ADS speed penalties

Bug fixes

  • Iron sights no longer clip into the 2.50x optic on the VCR-2
  • Z84M pull tab now displays correctly during reload animations
  • Equipping the Standard Suppressor on the L115 applies the intended hip-fire accuracy penalty
  • Corrected the attachment point cost for the Frangible ammunition on MK24
  • Resolved attachment 3D preview misalignment for VSSM and LMR27 in Loadouts
