Valheim Patch 1.0.21

Valheim Patch 1.0.21 has been applied, featuring various bug fixes and adjustments. Upload schedules may vary by platform, but crossplay remains fully operational.

Key changes

  • Fixed an issue where jumping onto certain enemies caused players to bounce away at high speeds
  • Fixed storms from driving indoors
  • Changed certain food crafting tables and adjusted recipes
  • Removed the breeding capability of Wolf Spirits
  • Reduced Kall Fimbulbringer's damage-over-time fire damage and screen shake
  • Fixed an issue where multi-hit damage was reduced for weapons like the Nordic Greatsword

Buffs

  • Adjusted the Nordic Greatsword and derivative weapons to deal proper damage when striking multiple enemies simultaneously
  • Halved the Yagluth Aspect damage-over-time fire damage for Kall Fimbulbringer
  • Resolved an issue where certain boss and death markers could not be filtered using a gamepad

Nerfs

  • Removed the Wolf Spirit breeding feature
  • Reduced camera shake during Kall Fimbulbringer's attacks (adjusted)

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug where jumping on top of enemies caused high-speed bouncing
  • Fixed blizzards and ash storms penetrating into houses
  • Fixed an issue where the trader GUI would not open under certain circumstances
  • Fixed a bug where two accessories could be equipped simultaneously
  • Fixed an issue where Kall Fimbulbringer's loot dropped beneath the floor
