November 19 Update Information

A new 5-star hero and artifact, 'Bunny Girl Maid Cecilia' and 'Secret Mission', have been added. Additionally, 'Chaos Call' Season 2 has begun, along with updates such as preparations for Arena Season 2027-1.

Key changes

  • Added new 5-star hero 'Bunny Girl Maid Cecilia' and artifact 'Secret Mission'
  • Held 'Chaos Call' Season 2 (2026-11-19 ~ 2026-12-10)
  • Relaxed entry and Draft Arena account level restrictions for Chaos Call from 40 to 20
  • Changed hero lineup and adjusted Fate balance for Chaos Call Season 2
  • Expanded Chaos Call season ranking reward eligibility from top 100 to top 1000

Buffs

  • Increased support hero stats and optimized equipment settings
  • Buffed and adjusted 24 Epic Fates, 6 Hero-grade Fates, and 4 Rare-grade Fates

Nerfs

  • Epic Fates: Nerfed certain fates including Landmark (Your Under Arrest) and Arbiter Vildred
  • Hero-grade Fates: Nerfed Oath of Silence
  • Rare-grade Fates: Nerfed Destroyer's Expert
