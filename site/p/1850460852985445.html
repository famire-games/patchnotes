Hearts of Iron IV Patch 1.20.1 & Studio Gold Update

Patch 1.20.1 brings balance tweaks to Balkan mechanics and bug fixes for the Romanian focus tree. Additionally, the Studio Gold update introduces a major multiplayer networking overhaul using AI tools to combat cheaters and griefers.

Key changes

  • Reduced extra non-core manpower and compliance gains for certain Romanian national spirits and advisors
  • Improved focus tree branch hiding and monarchist diplomacy availability for Romania
  • Reworked networking layer and command validation in the Clausewitz engine using AI assistance
  • Deleted nearly 20,000 lines of old code and updated 400+ game commands to prevent exploits
  • Implemented automatic player removal and lobby notification for unauthorized command manipulation

Buffs

  • Starting National Spirit 'Romanian Armed Forces' now correctly affects unit HP, and 'Improve Army Medical Care' correctly modifies it

Nerfs

  • Reduced 'Defender of the Balkans' extra non-core manpower from 25% to 7.5%
  • Reduced compliance gain on 'Protector of the Orthodox' from +0.25% to +0.15%
  • Reduced compliance gain from Romanian advisor Dimitrie Gusti from +0.05% to +0.02%

Bug fixes

  • Romanian subjects will no longer refuse Balkan Union integration
  • Removed a duplicate country leader check from Iuliu Maniu
  • Fixed 'A Show of Force' focus checking the wrong flag and never unlocking
