Patch 36.2.2 Notes

A balance update has been applied to Hearthstone and Battlegrounds, nerfing several overperforming cards and buffing underperforming ones. Additionally, multiple Battlegrounds-related bugs have been fixed.

Key changes

  • Adjusted meta-defining cards such as Warden Maiev, Galakrond's Henchman, and Murgurgle
  • Nerfed Tol'vir Adversary, Arcane Wire Trap, and Jade Protectors
  • Buffed Lotus Boss Aya, Blazing Incantation, Soul Sacrifice, and more
  • Fixed multiple Battlegrounds-related bugs

Buffs

  • Lotus Boss Aya: Buffed to grant 3 Counterfeit Coins
  • Blazing Incantation: Added an effect to reduce the Cost of discovered minions by (1)
  • Soul Sacrifice: Mana Cost reduced from 3 to 2
  • Rouser: Added Taunt ability
  • Vulkanos: Increased damage and health
  • Staff of Schemes: Increased health

Nerfs

  • Warden Maiev: Health reduced from 4 to 3
  • Galakrond's Henchman: Mana Cost increased from 1 to 2, stats adjusted
  • Murgurgle: Hero Power unlocks on turn 3
  • Tol'vir Adversary: Mana Cost reduced to 4 and effect restricted to minions
  • Arcane Wire Trap: Damage reduced from 5 to 4
  • Jade Protectors: Mana Cost increased from 3 to 5

Bug fixes

  • Fixed visual effect delay and duplicate playback for Quiet Deliverer
  • Fixed Deathrattle tooltip error for Butcher's Sickle
  • Fixed legend border display error for Enterprising Escapist
  • Fixed text and effect errors related to Gift of the Dark
  • Fixed minion summon timing error for Sneed's Hero Power
