Baldur's Gate 3 · September 24, 2025

Hotfix #34 Released

A native Steam Deck build has been introduced to Baldur's Gate 3, providing stable framerates and improved loading times. Intel XeSS support has been added, and various crashes and bugs have been fixed.

Key changes

  • Added native Steam Deck build for improved performance and framerate stability
  • Added support for Intel's new super-sampling technology (XeSS)
  • Fixed potential crashes related to the Slow condition and summoning 'Owlbear Cub / Scratch' in combat
  • Fixed various crashes and Honour Mode loading issues on Xbox platforms
  • Fixed an issue where minions (Hounds of Ill Omen, Conjured Undead) had their HP affected by enemy difficulty scaling
  • Fixed a bug where the Apostle of Myrkul regenerated HP when loading a save during combat in Honour / Tactician mode

Bug fixes

  • Fixed potential crashes that occurred when applying the Slow condition or casting the 'Find Familiar' spell during combat
  • Fixed Xbox crashes occurring when pausing and resuming during cinematics and when loading Honour Mode saves
  • Fixed a bug where the Apostle of Myrkul regenerated HP when loading a save during combat in Tactician and Honour modes
  • Fixed an issue where the HP of Hounds of Ill Omen and Conjured Undead was affected by enemy difficulty scaling
  • Fixed an audio loop bug where the sound of dashing True Strike would repeat infinitely
