Patch 36.6.3 Notes (Battlegrounds)

This patch focuses on balancing by weakening the anomaly system to reduce early-game tempo, and buffing various underperforming minions, tavern spells, and trinkets. Additionally, guaranteed appearance rates and high appearance rates for new minion types and trinkets have been ended early and returned to normal levels.

Key changes

  • Increased the minion death requirement for Anomaly's Embodiment from 3 to 4, and moved all embodiments to Tier 1
  • Ended guaranteed appearance periods for new minion types and trinkets and normalized appearance rates
  • Buffed and nerfed various minions, tavern spells, and trinkets
  • Restricted the appearance of Duos battlegrounds minions and certain heroes in anomaly lobbies
  • Changed costs and effects of Dark Gifts and certain trinkets
