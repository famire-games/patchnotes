Ranked Beta Season 2 Start and System Overhaul

Ranked Beta Season 1 has concluded and Beta Season 2 has begun in Deadlock. This season introduces a soft ranked reset and global redistribution, reduced ranked protection shields, increased hero bans, and revised hero unlock requirements.

Key changes

  • Beta Season 2 begins (runs until March 11, 2027)
  • Applied an average 1-badge soft reset and global rank redistribution
  • Removed ranked protection shields for Phantom, Ascendant, and Eternus tiers, and reduced them for other tiers
  • Increased ranked points earned per match from 300 to 350
  • Increased hero bans per ranked match from 3 to 4
  • Changed ranked hero unlock requirements from a win-based system to a win/loss point (35 pts) system

Buffs

  • Increased ranked points earned per match from 300 to 350
  • Reduced points required to promote to a new badge in the final sub-rank from 2000 to 1500

Nerfs

  • Removed ranked protection shields for Phantom, Ascendant, and Eternus tiers
  • Reduced full rank border protection shields from 5 to 3 for other tiers
  • Reduced sub-rank protection shields from 2 to 1 for other tiers

Bug fixes

  • Conducted backend rework to fix numerous bugs related to rank leaderboards
