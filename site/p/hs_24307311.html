Patch 37.0.2 Notes

Balance updates have been applied to Hearthstone, Arena, and Battlegrounds. The abilities of the Old God 'C'thun' have been buffed, cards with excessive early-game power have been nerfed, and many underperforming cards and strategies have been buffed. In addition, some Wild card adjustments, Arena balance patches, and bug fixes were made.

Key changes

  • C'thun's abilities increased from 3x to 4x, strengthening late-game rewards
  • Nerfed many cards with strong early-game Battlegrounds board control such as Warlock and Priest
  • Buffed Old Gods other than C'thun as well as underperforming cards and strategies
  • Restricted synergies and adjusted cards related to Wild's Yogg-Saron, Hope's End
  • Adjusted class appearance rates and balance patches in Arena
  • Fixed some achievements experience errors and Arena card appearance errors
