V70 - Incubating Update

The Lethal Company V70 patch has been applied. The radar system has been completely overhauled, the mansion interior has been reworked, and new furniture and daytime exterior entities have been added.

Key changes

  • Radar overhaul adding a facecam to view teammates' faces from the terminal
  • Added new furniture including refrigerators, armchairs, and electric chairs
  • Added 'Giant Sapsucker', a new daytime exterior entity commonly found on Vow
  • Mansion interior rework with new rooms and interactable items
  • Company Cruiser improved to better withstand tree and rock collisions with a self-righting feature
  • Added Sigurd's new log 'Team Synergy'

Buffs

  • Enhanced the material of the Company Cruiser to better withstand crashes into trees and rocks
  • Added a self-leveling feature to the Company Cruiser
  • Stepping on the pedal after changing gears produces a small boost
  • Slightly increased the spawn probability of Zed Dog, the rarest scrap

Bug fixes

  • Various minor bug fixes
