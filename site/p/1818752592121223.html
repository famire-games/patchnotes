Garry's Mod December 2025 Patch

A small patch has been released to address regressions from the November Update alongside several minor fixes. Key changes include Combine Mine relationship adjustments, an overhaul of the player kick system, and crash fixes for saves and corrupted maps. Sandbox servers and clients are advised to update promptly.

Key changes

  • Combine Mine relationship adjustments (respects AI settings, updated target logic)
  • Reimplemented kickid console command and unified player kick internals
  • Fixed decal rendering and re-added model scale to prop_dynamic in Hammer
  • Updated Zombine and unarmored Combine Soldier player model animations
  • Limited physics rescaling to prop_physics and sent_anim

Bug fixes

  • Fixed certain NPCs like Alyx and Citizens firing 2 bullets per pistol shot
  • Fixed crash when loading Source Engine saves containing Nextbots
  • Fixed map load crashes associated with corrupted overlays
  • Fixed floating addons failing to mount on Linux systems
  • Fixed prop gibs stopping spawning after the 300th in multiplayer
