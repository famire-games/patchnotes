Granblue Fantasy: Relink · August 28, 2026

Ver. 2.0.5 Update Information

The Ver. 2.0.5 update for Granblue Fantasy: Relink has been released, bringing character adjustments, quest reward increases, and various bug fixes.

Key changes

  • Adjusted Fediel's 'Insight: Finishing Touch' max effect level and magic orb damage
  • Increased MSP rewards for clearing several Chaos++ and Defy Infinity quests
  • Increased the MSP value cap to 9,999,999
  • Fixed and reset issues related to limited-supply Conflux treasure bundles
  • Fixed camera issues and softlocks during specific online co-op quests

Buffs

  • Increased MSP awarded for clearing various Chaos++ quests and all Defy Infinity quests

Nerfs

  • Reduced Fediel's max level of Enhanced Combo Finishers from 10 to 5, and lowered magic orb damage without the effect

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug allowing Gallanza to activate Skybound Art without a full SBA gauge
  • Fixed intermittent camera malfunctions and softlocks in quests like The Not Forgotten Sky
  • Fixed a discrepancy between wee pincer counts in Key Items and Chapter Select
  • Fixed a bug allowing players to equip master traits beyond the equip limit
