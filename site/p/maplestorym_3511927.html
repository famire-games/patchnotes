Challengers World and New Boss Kalo Update

'Challengers World', a limited-time special world supporting rapid growth, has opened, and the new boss 'Wanderer of Time Kalo' has been added. In addition, various events such as Challengers World-exclusive missions, burning, and World Leap are being held concurrently.

Key changes

  • Opened limited-time 'Challengers World' (7/30–9/30)
  • Added Challengers World-exclusive new boss 'Wanderer of Time Kalo'
  • Introduced 'World Leap' system to transfer Challengers World characters to existing worlds
  • Held 'Challengers Hyper Burning' to support rapid leveling up to Lv. 230
  • Held 'Challengers Item Burning' event to support equipment growth
  • Held Challengers Hall of Fame and various commemorative events
