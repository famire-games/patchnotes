Snowbreak New Update: Defiant Bloom

A special main story chapter 'Defiant Bloom' and various limited-time events have begun. Pickup events and shops for the new operator Clarinus (Crimson Moon) and various weapons are now open. Enjoy new gameplay experiences such as co-op and challenges.

Key changes

  • Opened main story special chapter 'Defiant Bloom' and corresponding event shop
  • Held pickup for new operator 'Clarinus - Crimson Moon' and weapon 'Rosewing'
  • Held selective pickups for operators (Yao, Mauxir, Enya) and weapons (Starfall, etc.)
  • Rerun event story 'Verdurous Holiday' and mini-games
  • Sequentially opened various challenge and co-op content (Supreme Battleground, Defense Line Zero, etc.)
