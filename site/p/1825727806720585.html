War Robots 11.8 Update

The 'Mercenary Town' event arrives featuring the new robot Anaksor, new weapons, the Myia drone, and the Ultimate Revenant build. Numerous balance adjustments for robots, titans, weapons, and drones, along with technical fixes, have also been implemented.

Key changes

  • New robot Anaksor and weapons Velos, Machaira, and Basileus
  • New Myia drone
  • Ultimate Revenant, Ultimate Scatter, and Ultimate Devastator added
  • New pilots Alex Tav and Valerie Morgan
  • Extensive balance adjustments across robots, titans, weapons, and drones

Buffs

  • Rex durability and built-in weapon damage increased
  • Kaji durability increased
  • Typhon Aegis capacity increased
  • Angler physical shield durability increased
  • Buffs to Titans such as Minos, Heimdall, and Sirius

Nerfs

  • Teth durability and forcefield durability decreased
  • Gladius and Pilum damage decreased
  • Flux damage decreased
  • Keel mothership damage decreased

Bug fixes

  • Beholder: BLAST will now deal direct damage and AoE damage as intended
