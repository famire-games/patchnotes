November 12 Update

The November 12 update for Delta Force brings operator balance adjustments for Martelo, S12 Fate Contracts difficulty changes, Warfare map adjustments for Zenith Port, and various bug fixes.

Key changes

  • Adjusted Martelo's Smoke Halo sound range and camera rotation audio
  • Easier completion requirements for S12 Fate Contracts: Expert Handler
  • Zenith Port deployment base and vehicle supply adjustments
  • Vehicle Supply Stations now share a simultaneous cooldown
  • Added S12 - Aurora tracks to the lobby music player
  • Fixed issues regarding pings through smoke and Gunsmithing in Warfare

Buffs

  • Reduced Martelo's Ariete hit requirement from 6 to 3 in Expert Handler
  • Adjusted Rover's contract task requirements for easier completion

Nerfs

  • Increased sound attenuation beyond 30 meters for Martelo's Smoke Halo
  • All Vehicle Supply Stations enter cooldown simultaneously after servicing

Bug fixes

  • Fixed abnormal damage to characters and vehicles after bus explosions
  • Fixed an issue where Gunsmith could not be unlocked in Warfare
  • Fixed standard and Rover command pings not passing through smoke
  • Fixed Weapon Crates yielding expired limited-time Attachments
  • Fixed a delay when aiming down sights with a controller
