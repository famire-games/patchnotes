Cities: Skylines II · November 10, 2026

Patch 1.6.4f1 - Seasonal Shift

Patch 1.6.4f1 for Cities: Skylines II is now live. It lays the groundwork for the upcoming Park Adventures DLC, brings Open Beta improvements to the live game, and includes Paradox Mods enhancements and bug fixes.

Key changes

  • Unlock the Grand Post Office signature building by subscribing to the Paradox Newsletter
  • Brings Open Beta improvements and updated upscalers (DLSS 4.5, FSR 3, XeSS 3)
  • Major improvements to Paradox Mods navigation and features
  • Fixed performance issues caused by cities building up too many companies with no buildings
  • Fixed the Hydroelectric Power Plant not functioning

Bug fixes

  • Fixed cities building up too many companies with no buildings causing performance issues
  • Fixed the Hydroelectric Power Plant not functioning
  • Fixed trees with growth turned off continuing to grow
  • Fixed mod content sometimes not loading on startup
  • Fixed game mode image flickering when hovered in the menu
