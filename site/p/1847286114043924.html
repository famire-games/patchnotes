DAVE THE DIVER · October 26, 2026

October 26 Patch Notes

Following Steam's DLC achievement management method change, the 'Into the Jungle' DLC achievements have been added. Achievement completion rates for the main game and DLC are now separated and can be viewed individually in the Steam Client Beta, and previously achieved achievements in the in-game smartphone will be synchronized with Steam achievements upon loading save files.

Key changes

  • Windows version v1.0.6.2118 and Mac version v1.0.6.761 applied
  • Steam achievement sets applied and added for owners of the 'Into the Jungle' content pack
  • Main game and DLC achievement completion rates can be tracked separately in Steam Client Beta
  • Achievements completed in the existing in-game smartphone achievement app automatically synchronize with Steam achievements
