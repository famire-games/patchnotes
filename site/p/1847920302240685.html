Don't Starve Together · October 30, 2026

Hotfix 761239 Update

The aggro sharing mechanics for Franken swine, Mermimids, and Vampire monsters have been changed. Various bugs have been fixed, including Willow's shadow fires and Terramite targeting.

Key changes

  • Franken swine, Mermimids, and Vampire monsters no longer automatically share targets upon individual aggro
  • Fixed a bug where Willow's shadow fires would only circle around targets
  • Fixed a bug where Terramites could not be targeted
  • Fixed a bug where Franken swine, Mermimids, and Vampire monsters incorrectly interacted with Void Masques
  • Fixed a bug where Moonbound Abigail's dash attack went outside the shrine area
  • Fixed a bug where Moonbound Crows got stuck in attack animations near walls

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug where Willow's shadow fires would circle without reaching the target
  • Fixed a bug where Terramites could not be targeted
  • Fixed a bug where Franken swine, Mermimids, and Vampire monsters incorrectly interacted with Void Masques
  • Fixed a bug where Moonbound Abigail's dash attack went outside the shrine and shrine lobby
  • Fixed a bug where Moonbound Crows would get stuck repeating their attack motion near walls
