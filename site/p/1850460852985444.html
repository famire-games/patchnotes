The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Patch 5.02

Patch 5.02 for The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt is now live. This update brings gameplay and balance adjustments, visual improvements, photo mode updates, and numerous bug fixes across the Continent.

Key changes

  • Gameplay and balance adjustments
  • Skill and mutation balance updates
  • Photo Mode improvements and fixes
  • PC Path Tracing and Ray Reconstruction optimizations
  • Added support for 32:9 super-ultrawide aspect ratio

Buffs

  • Delayed Recovery can now also be used with Decoctions

Nerfs

  • Catalyst: Reduced Aard and Igni intensity bonus by 10%
  • Sustained Glyphs: Reduced Sign duration bonus by 3 seconds
  • Adrenaline Rush: Reduced Attack Power and Sign Intensity bonus by 20%
  • Cat Eyes: Reduced damage bonus by 50
  • Magic Sensibilities: Reduced critical hit chance by 15% and critical hit damage by 50%
  • Mutated Skin: Lowered damage reduction by 5% per Adrenaline Point
  • Piercing Cold: Reduced damage bonus by 500

Bug fixes

  • Fixed Zoltan Chivay Gwent card spawning under Hanged Man's Tree
  • Fixed empty inventory bug caused by installing/uninstalling mods
  • Fixed New Game+ Aerondight damage value reset and item level requirement issues
  • Fixed meditation time going backwards indefinitely
