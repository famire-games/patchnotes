Rocket League Season 23 Update

Rocket League Season 23 kicks off on June 10, introducing a FIFA World Cup 26 collaboration event and the new United Futura arena. Demo respawn position control and various quality-of-life features have been added, alongside new Rocket Pass Premium vehicles.

Key changes

  • Rocket League Season 23 opens on June 10 with the FIFA World Cup 26 collaboration
  • Hosted a World Cup Limited-Time Event (LTE) to earn national team decals and titles
  • Added the new arena United Futura
  • Introduced Demo Spawn Control allowing players to choose their respawn direction after being demolished
  • Added various convenience and custom features reflecting popular community requests
  • Acquire the Lyra Trophy and Lyra T60 vehicles through Rocket Pass Premium
