Teamfight Tactics Patch 18.6 Notes

With the introduction of the Teamfight Tactics standalone client, in-game convenience improvements along with balance adjustments for certain traits, units, and augments have been made. Set 19 commemorative thank-you missions, reward plans, and various bug fixes have been applied.

Key changes

  • Introduced Teamfight Tactics standalone client (large download size)
  • Set 19 commemorative thank-you missions and rewards to be distributed
  • Buffed Bloom, Elderwood, Swiftshot traits and Kobuko, Rek'Sai, Dusk Wolf, Malphite, Nidalee, Warden units
  • Buffed Clockwork Lubricant, Heroic-sized Effort, Spilling, Beyond Competition augments and buffed/nerfed Growing Caterpillars
  • Increased cost of Gilded Cart emblems and mid-game costs
  • Reintroduced streamer mode and improved chat window readability

Buffs

  • Increased Bloom attack power/ability power
  • Increased Swiftshot Huge and Fluffy Friends ranged rider attack power/ability power
  • Increased Kobuko skill health healing
  • Increased Rek'Sai skill health healing
  • Increased Dusk Wolf empowered basic attack damage
  • Increased Malphite shield amount and skill damage
  • Increased Nidalee attack power/ability power form health healing and damage
  • Increased Warden shield amount health scaling
  • Increased Clockwork Lubricant attack speed per activation
  • Increased Heroic-sized Effort base armor and magic resistance
  • Increased Spilling initial gold gain
  • Increased Beyond Competition Rengar health regen sharing and Kha'Zix mana sharing

Nerfs

  • Reduced Elderwood (9) tier bonus health
  • Reduced Growing Caterpillars bonus attack power/ability power
  • Increased Gilded Cart emblem cost
  • Increased mid-game and upgrade costs

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug where default settings were set low on some new devices
  • Fixed a bug where PC resolution settings did not work when not in windowed mode
  • Fixed a bug where Jeweled Lotus provided more ability power than intended
  • Fixed a bug where items changed positions on the bench after using certain consumables
  • Fixed an issue so Flexible and U.R.F. cannot be selected together
