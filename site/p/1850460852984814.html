Dragonfire Patch 5

This patch brings Season 35 extensions and Crystal League updates, Dragon Sanctuary and Armory improvements, and various combat balance adjustments for weapons and armors.

Key changes

  • Season 35 is extended by three weeks with updates to the Conqueror's Challenge and Crystal League.
  • Loot inside Azaryon’s chest has doubled, and new Armory categories for Ancient Lands and Dragon Sanctuary were added.
  • A quick spell-change option has been added by right-clicking spell slots.
  • All Cloth Armor now grants 15% resilience penetration.
  • Combat balance adjustments were made to Arcane Staffs, Axes, Bows, Crossbows, Daggers, and more.

Buffs

  • Conqueror’s Chest rewards increased by approximately 50%
  • Azaryon’s chest overall loot value doubled
  • Evensong (Arcane Staffs) added DoT and increased slow
  • Whirlwind (Greataxe) damage scaling updated to continue hitting multiple targets
  • Well of Life (Wild Staff) added an instant heal
  • All Cloth Armor added 15% resilience penetration

Nerfs

  • Lucent Hawk (Mistpiercer) damage reduced
  • Flaming Phoenix (Dawnsong) range reduced
  • Holy Flash (all Holy Staffs) range reduced
  • Displacement Immunity (Knight Helmet) duration reduced
  • Wings of Fire (Dragonslayer Jacket) damage bonus removed

Bug fixes

  • Fixed issue where Rejuvenation (Nature Staffs) interrupted movement when casting
  • Fixed issue where Dragon Sanctuary PvE stats were not tracked on the leaderboard
  • Fixed issue where items with a Quality higher than normal could not be stacked by clicking-and-dragging
  • Fixed issue where HUD Tracker lost Destiny Board elements and could not be collapsed after changing regions
  • Fixed issue where Sentry Mages could be damaged before a Territory battle
