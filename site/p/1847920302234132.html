Update 12.1.0

This update adjusts the Translator and weapon balance, introduces the Halloween event, and includes various bug fixes and UI quality-of-life improvements.

Key changes

  • Translator maximum range reduced from 70m to 50m
  • CB-01 Repeater damage reduced from 88 to 85
  • Pike-556 headshot multiplier reduced from 1.75 to 1.5
  • Shotgun pellet hitmarker duration reduced and option to disable added
  • Visual effects and fog visibility improved on maps like Monaco and Skyway Stadium
  • Fixed bugs related to the Mimic Device and Tracking Dart

Nerfs

  • Reduced Translator maximum range (70m -> 50m)
  • Reduced CB-01 Repeater damage (88 -> 85)
  • Reduced Pike-556 headshot multiplier (1.75 -> 1.5)

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where daily and weekly contracts could not be progressed in Cashout (Bot)
  • Fixed bugs related to transparent collision and shadows when using the Mimic Device
  • Fixed an issue where weapons would fire after the Tracking Dart ammo was consumed
  • Fixed an issue where statues could get stuck out of bounds
  • Fixed a visual issue where the concrete canopy skin appeared gray
