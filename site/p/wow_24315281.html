WoW: Forever November Hotfixes

Emergency hotfixes for World of Warcraft: Forever deployed from November 11 to 14, 2026. Includes class skill and talent balance adjustments, dungeon experience and mob difficulty updates, and numerous bug fixes for items and quests.

Key changes

  • Druid Wild Growth healing coefficient increased and movement casting bug fixed
  • Paladin Judgment calculation split into ranged and melee attacks
  • Shaman Windfury Totem and Maelstrom Weapon mechanics overhauled
  • Warlock Affliction Destruction skill functionality changed
  • Dungeon monster kill experience uniformly increased by 50%
  • High-level quest reward overhaul, including removing the feature that replaced late-level quest rewards with gold just before level 60

Buffs

  • Druid: Wild Growth healing coefficient increased to 6.6% (from 3.3%)
  • Shaman: Weapon enhancements and weapon totems can now be used simultaneously
  • Warlock: Base health of Imp, Voidwalker, Succubus, and Incubus increased by approximately 15%
  • Warrior: Sweeping Strikes no longer loses a charge if it fails to hit an additional target

Nerfs

  • Shaman: Stormstrike damage reduced to 100% of weapon damage and cooldown increased to 8 seconds
  • Warlock: Destruction damage reduced by 30%
  • Sunken City: Respawn time for enemies other than bosses increased to 2 hours (from 1 hour)

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug where players who learned Talent 2 had their action bar settings reset upon logging out
  • Fixed an issue where Berserker Rage did not remove stun effects
  • Fixed an issue preventing normal interaction with the command tent during the Western Plaguelands quest 'The Scarlet Diversion'
  • Fixed an issue where the audience chamber door failed to open after a wipe during the Emperor Dagran Thaurissan encounter in Blackrock Depths
  • Fixed a bug where all players' unread mail appeared to be sent from the Stormwind Auction House
