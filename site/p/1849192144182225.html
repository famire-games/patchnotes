Marvel Rivals Balance Patch Notes

Marvel Rivals has implemented major balance adjustments targeting Vanguard and Duelist heroes. The performance of characters like Angela and Groot has been buffed, while Devil Dinosaur, Doctor Strange, Emma Frost, and others have been nerfed.

Key changes

  • Buffed Angela's ground combat power and buff duration
  • Nerfed Devil Dinosaur's survivability and CC duration
  • Adjusted Doctor Strange's shield value and team-up effect
  • Adjusted Emma Frost's max health-scaling damage
  • Buffed Black Cat's Fortune gain and Black Panther team-up
  • Nerfed Elsa Bloodstone's long-range poke capability

Buffs

  • Angela: Increased first 3-hit damage (30->35), changed Divine Judgment buff duration (6s)
  • Groot: Increased Thorny Whip wall Flora Colossus effect damage (60->65)
  • Rogue: Increased Power Surge Punch first two hit damage (35->40)
  • Black Cat: Increased Feline Fury maximum Fortune gain (50->75)
  • Black Widow: Increased Electro Plasma Blast damage (50-70 -> 55-75)

Nerfs

  • Devil Dinosaur: Reduced Savage Predation control and knock-up duration
  • Doctor Strange: Reduced Seraphim Shield value (850->800) and recovery speed
  • Emma Frost: Reduced Mind Aegis shield value (400->350)
  • Peni Parker: Reduced Cyber-Web Cluster explosion damage (15->12)
  • Elsa Bloodstone: Adjusted Double Barrel Blaster damage drop-off distance (starts at 20m -> 10m)
