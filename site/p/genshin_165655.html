Version 7.0 "The Snowland Untouched by the Gods" Update

Version 7.0 of Genshin Impact opens up the new region Snezhnaya and introduces a new elemental reaction, 'Stellar Diffusion', along with a host of new characters and equipment. Furthermore, various new content and systems such as a third-person shooting mode and base building have been introduced.

Key changes

  • Opened the new region 'Snezhnaya' and its associated exploration systems
  • Added new characters 'Odette', 'Traveler (Cryo)', and 'Alyosha'
  • Added the new elemental reaction 'Stellar Diffusion' and the special weapon 'Starlight Sword'
  • Added new equipment (limited weapons, forged weapons, Battle Pass weapons, and 2 new artifact sets)
  • Introduced new content including 'Third-Person Shooting Mode' and 'Ashen Pupil' quality-of-life features
  • Updated Spiral Abyss, Imaginarium Theater, and Genius Invokation TCG content
