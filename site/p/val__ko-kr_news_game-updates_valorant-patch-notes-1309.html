VALORANT Patch Notes 13.09

A new Agent, Hesper, equipped with telekinesis skills has been released, and Warden's weapon performance has been buffed. In addition, skill descriptions and tooltip consistency have been improved, and official terms for positive (Boost) and negative (Targeted) effects have been introduced.

Key changes

  • New Agent 'Hesper' joins (equipped with Crowd Disruption, Flashbang, Torrent, and Total Halt skills)
  • Warden weapon buff (smoother vertical recoil for the first few shots and reduced spread for follow-up shots)
  • Official terms introduced for positive effect 'Boost' and negative effect 'Targeted', alongside tooltip UI overhaul
  • Adjusted Clove's vitality recovery amount and changed the 'Not Dead Yet' kill reward rules
  • Improved competitive performance score convenience and changed map tier calculation methods
  • Added in-game Radianite Point purchase function exclusively for Korean servers

Buffs

  • Warden: The first few shots of firing have smoother vertical recoil, and the spread error of the second and third bullets is reduced.

Nerfs

  • Clove: The reduced movement speed bonus from vitality recovery was adjusted from 15% to 10%.

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug where Clove's Ruse tactical map was visible to enemies and the vitality recovery timer display bug
  • Fixed a bug where Cypher's Cyber Cage changed direction when placed on a sloped surface
  • Fixed a bug where capture voice lines did not play for enemies trapped by Deadlock's Annihilation
  • Fixed a bug where Gekko's Mosh Pit dealt damage to destructible objects outside the intended range
  • Fixed a bug where Omen's From the Shadows arrival visual effects disappeared too quickly
