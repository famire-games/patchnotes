Cerulean Getaway Update

The new limited-time event 'Cerulean Getaway' and new operator 'Siris - Peredhel' have been added. Various combat modes, mini-games, and pickup events will run sequentially.

Key changes

  • Opened 'Cerulean Getaway' limited event story and shop
  • Opened special invitation event for new operator 'Siris - Peredhel' (available to obtain for free)
  • Held limited-time Echo pickups for Fenny - Coronet and Siris - Peredhel
  • Pre-opened new main story prologue and Chapter 14 Pt.2
  • Added various mini-games and co-op content (Anchors Aweigh, Dreamboard Harbor, etc.)
  • Refreshed challenge content including Starward Defense and Paradox Labyrinth
