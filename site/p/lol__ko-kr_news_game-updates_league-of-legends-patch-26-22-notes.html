League of Legends Patch 26.22 Notes

League of Legends patch 26.22 has been applied. Mel mid-scope update, official separation of Teamfight Tactics into a standalone client, the addition of classic mode new champions (Volibear, Swain, Elise), and various champion and item balance adjustments have been made.

Key changes

  • Teamfight Tactics (TFT) officially migrated to a standalone client
  • Applied Mel mid-scope update (changed abilities and growth stats)
  • ARAM: Chaos augments and Butcher's Bridge, Koeshin Bridge maps rejoin
  • Added old versions of Volibear, Swain, and Elise to LoL Classic
  • Buffed pro-play-oriented champions and nerfed high-ban-rate champions
  • Increased Classic Pass token acquisition amount by 50%

Buffs

  • Aurelion Sol (Buffed E skill)
  • Corki (Increased R skill damage)
  • Kayle (Reduced Q and R cooldowns)
  • Maokai (Increased Q skill max health damage scaling)
  • Naafiri (Buffed Q and W skills)
  • Rumble (Increased R skill damage)
  • Shen (Increased Q monster damage scaling)
  • Smolder (Increased W explosion damage)
  • Viktor (Increased health growth and R initial damage)
  • Zaheen (Reduced Q cooldown)
  • Overlord's Bloodmail (Increased attack damage)
  • Evelynn (Increased Q base damage)
  • Ezreal (Increased W, E AP scaling and healing)
  • Gangplank (Increased base health regen, health growth, Q mana cost, etc.)
  • Nami (Increased Q damage and W bounce damage reduction)
  • Olaf (Increased W bonus attack power and reduced E cooldown)

Nerfs

  • Roc (Nerfed Q and W skills)
  • Thresh (Reduced base attack power and R damage)
  • Yasuo (Reduced Q damage)
  • Doran's Blade (Reduced attack power)
  • Hecarim (Removed ability haste)
  • Fiddlesticks (Reduced E damage per hit)
  • Janna (Reduced passive map-wide movement speed)
  • Mordekaiser (Removed bonus damage against inhibitors/nexus)
  • Rammus (Reduced base armor and attack power per level)
  • Amumu (Reduced passive magic resistance reduction, increased Q damage and R cooldown)

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug where players could not move when using Cleanse/Flash equivalents (Jinx) after being killed by the fountain turret
  • Fixed a bug where Jeweled Gauntlet did not provide the correct critical strike chance from mages and adaptive force
  • Fixed a bug where Aatrox's R - World Ender basic attacks did not play the critical strike animation
  • Fixed a bug where Master Yi's passive second strike ignored blindness
  • Fixed a bug where Sivir's E - Spell Shield failed to block Ezreal's Q
