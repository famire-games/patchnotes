NARAKA: BLADEPOINT · November 18, 2026

November 19 Update Notice

Naraka: Bladepoint added various new events and content, such as polar exploration and fishing, in the November 19 update. Additionally, hero Tael adjustments, weapon and armor balance patches, a new mode, and new items were introduced.

Key changes

  • Held Antarctic exploration-themed 'The Polar Expedition' and fishing event 'Joy of Fishing'
  • Added 10 new counter items and new stages/maps
  • Adjusted hero 'Tael's ultimates 'Maw of Ruin' and 'Maw of Ruin: Void'
  • Adjusted balance for various weapons, armors, Souljades, and accessories
  • Introduced new gameplay method with the Conch Boat
  • Opened J-Cup Esports tournament commemoration event and tournament support mini-game

Buffs

  • Increased Monarch's Roar basic attack damage
  • Increased Unyielding Rock Armor starting defense
  • Increased Calamity Ward Armor and Darksteel Armor starting defense
  • Increased Guardian Armor starting max health
  • Increased Taotie's Rush damage multiplier from 1.0 to 1.3

Nerfs

  • Decreased Maw of Ruin: Void left-click finisher damage multiplier from 4.0 to 3.6
  • Decreased weapon poison stack count for Poison Essence
  • Decreased freeze duration for White Tiger's Prowess

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where treasure chest and Soul-Linked Pillar chest locations overlapped on the Stormchant map
  • Fixed incorrectly displayed descriptions when triggering certain relic mysteries and season combat items
