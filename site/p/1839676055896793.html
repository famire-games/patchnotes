8/4 Server Maintenance and August Update Announcement

UTC 2026-08-04 23:00 ~ 08:00

Server maintenance is scheduled from August 4, 2026, at 11:00 PM to August 5, 2026, at 8:00 AM UTC. Players will be unable to log in, and campaigns or Scratch Tickets will be unavailable while quest counter updates, recipe helpers, and bug fixes are deployed.

Key changes

  • Replaced Limited-Time Quests with Recommended Quests and added recommended Element displays
  • Changed select attacks of Boss Enemy: Ruine Angel to be Guardable
  • Added the Recommended Recipe ingredient auto-selection feature to Quick Food
  • Added ability to copy expression settings across expressions in Character Creation
  • Added Granillusion Weapon Enhancement (Augment Transfer) Exchange Shop to Item Trader
  • Added Line Strike starter deck to the AC Shop and updated Card Scratch lineup

Bug fixes

  • Fixed lock-on breaking distance decreasing when incapacitated against Malignant Ruine Luther
  • Fixed processing load spikes during certain attacks of Boss Enemy: High Agni
  • Fixed issues preventing EX Tri-Shield series activation or causing cooldown resets upon area changes
  • Fixed a bug causing Line Strike matches to forcibly terminate under certain card effect timings
  • Fixed Potential: Triumphant Light effects failing to apply or reset correctly under specific conditions
