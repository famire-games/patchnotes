Patch 1.4.10 Update Notice

Patch 1.4.10 for The First Descendant adds transcendent weapon loot to 'Wall Crasher' defeat rewards, and improves visual effects and hit detection for certain Descendant skills such as Freyna and Monet. Furthermore, equipment and module errors such as EXCAVA Pulsar were fixed, and numerous UI/UX quality-of-life improvements were made.

Key changes

  • Added a chance to acquire transcendent weapon loot to Wall Crasher defeat rewards
  • Improved visual effects for Freyna and Ultimate Freyna's 'Toxic Stimulation' module 'Catalyst Zone'
  • Improved aiming angle correction for Monet's transcendent module 'Knight's Lance: Phase Separation'
  • Changed the 'Champion Vulgus' effect in certain areas of the Asylum to 'Legion Invincible' instead of 'Legion Recovery'
  • Expanded eligibility for the Weekly Content Benefit mission's 'Complete Weekly Content IV' challenge
  • Improved tooltip descriptions and amplification display formats for the trigger module 'Negentropy'

Buffs

  • Applied a transcendent weapon drop boost to Wall Crasher defeat rewards and added a chance to acquire 400-quality items

Bug fixes

  • Fixed issues with excessive visual effects and abnormal icon displays for Monet's skills
  • Fixed errors related to the EXCAVA Pulsar weapon's pulse bolt and overcharged singularity
  • Fixed an issue where the 'Research Acceleration' effect did not apply in Hagios Special Operation (Assembly: Hard)
  • Fixed an issue where players could not dismount vehicles and skills/jumping/weapon switching failed to function
  • Fixed Korean/English text mismatches in Battle Pass weekly challenges
