Teamfight Tactics Patch 18.7 Notes

The final patch of the Fabled Forest set ahead of the TFT Prism Tour. Trait, champion, and augment balances have been adjusted to revive former S-tier comps and enable creative off-meta builds, along with the return of macOS support.

Key changes

  • macOS support returns for M-series and MacBook devices
  • Internal queue system applied to the Reforger to prevent duplicate items
  • New Auto-detect quality mode introduced; separated Mobile Visual Effects and Resolution settings
  • Major trait and champion adjustments for Coven, Eclipse, Hellion, Primal, and more
  • Combat augment balance tweaks including Advance Pay and Dawn of the Comeback
  • Quality-of-life updates and bug fixes, including the ability to cancel Armory selections using the Esc key

Buffs

  • Increased essence gain at the highest Coven trait tier
  • Reduced kill interval requirement for Eclipse
  • Primal: Increased maximum component item cap from the Phoenix and reduced required takedowns
  • Skill buffs for units including Burning Sapling, Kobuko, Elise, Kayle, and Warwick
  • Increased Aphelios's Moonlight Wave damage

Nerfs

  • Elderwood (5) and Forest Guardian Health decreased
  • Faerie final tier gold reduced
  • Rift Beast (7) AD and AP decreased
  • Quickstriker bonus Health and Attack Speed decreased
  • Krug mini-krug base Health decreased

Bug fixes

  • Unconfirmed Armory selections can now be canceled using the Esc key
  • Opponent Health now updates properly after a victory
  • Fixed a stacking bug with Seeker's Armguard
  • Stored mana no longer carries over between combat rounds
  • Fixed a timer bug related to Augment Armories
