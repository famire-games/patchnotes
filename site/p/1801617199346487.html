V71 Bug Fix Patch

A bug fix patch for Lethal Company version V71, addressing several errors such as purchasable refrigerators and mansion interiors. It also improves visual effects on certain maps and enemy pathfinding issues.

Key changes

  • Fixed an issue where purchasable refrigerators could not be opened
  • Fixed floating lamps outside of Dine
  • Fixed an issue where the weed killer sound could be heard globally
  • Fixed collision issues with small stairs and railings inside the mansion
  • Gradually changed the fog display method on the Vow map
  • Slightly decreased the mine interior spawn probability on the Rend map

Buffs

  • Buffed birds so they can now attack players positioned at higher elevations

Nerfs

  • Slightly decreased the mine interior spawn probability on the Rend map

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where refrigerators could not be opened
  • Fixed floating lamps outside of Dine
  • Fixed global playback of weed killer sound
  • Fixed impassable mansion stairs and greenhouse doors
  • Fixed radar boosters being covered by large dots in commercial advertisements
