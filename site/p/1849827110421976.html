Singapore 2026 Major and Map Update

The PGL Singapore 2026 Major hub and Viewer Pass have been added, and the Cobblestone map has been added to official matchmaking. In addition, 8 Monstercat music kits and various animation fixes and bug patches have been applied.

Key changes

  • Released PGL Singapore 2026 Major hub and Viewer Pass
  • Added Cobblestone map to Casual, Competitive, Wingman, Retakes, and Deathmatch
  • Removed Boulder, Fachwerk, and Shelter maps from official matchmaking
  • Added 8 new music kits by Monstercat
  • Added custom processing for fire effect handling on ground materials
  • Added MVP Anthem preview button when inspecting music kits in the in-game store

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where the head was tucked too low at extreme aiming angles
  • Restored the lower aiming posture for the Terrorist faction
  • Fixed an issue where defuse kits were not correctly granted in Retake mode
  • Made adjustments so that Terrorist bots attack Counter-Terrorists attempting to defuse the bomb
