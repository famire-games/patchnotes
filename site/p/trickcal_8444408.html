November 12 Update Guide

Through regular maintenance on November 12, Trickcal Re:Dive kicks off a new season of Elias Frontier and adds a Main Story Season 3 bonus chapter along with new [Outing] content. Additionally, Trickcal Notes BETA team composition and card purchase preset sharing features have been introduced, alongside various quality-of-life improvements and bug fixes.

Key changes

  • Started Elias Frontier November regular season (changed boss and authority lists)
  • Added Main Story Season 3 bonus chapters (Episodes 1-6)
  • Added new [Outing] content (bag customization and badge decoration)
  • Added card purchase preset sharing and Trickcal Notes BETA [Team Composition] and [Leaderboard] features
  • Added settings to adjust combat effect intensity separately for allies and enemies
  • Added settings to improve Apostle resolution during story viewing

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where the lobby in use was not displayed as 'On Air' in the Highlight Lobby
  • Fixed an issue where newly acquired cards were incorrectly labeled as newly acquired when already owned
  • Fixed typos in Dudu Apostle's banquet hall preference notes
  • Fixed abnormal text display for the Dimension Clash next season preview
  • Fixed abnormal operation of the Weekday Farm disciple level condition filter
