GODDESS OF VICTORY: NIKKE · November 16, 2026

November 19 Update Summary

New Elysion SSR Nikke 'Sky' joins the Special Recruit pool, alongside the new story event 'SKY ON THE ROCK' and Champion Arena Regular Season 2. In addition, new and re-run costumes for Delta, Crown, and Modernia have been introduced along with various bug fixes and improvements.

Key changes

  • New SSR Nikke [Sky] added to Special Recruit
  • New story event [SKY ON THE ROCK] and Challenge Stages open
  • Limited Costume [Delta: Ninja Thief - Undercover Santa] added (December Mission Pass)
  • Costume Pick rerun for Limited Costumes [Crown: Naked King] and [Modernia: Second Affection]
  • Champion Arena Regular Season 2 kicks off and Coordinated Operation [Land Eater] begins
  • Archive [GO! NINJA THIEF!] added; new profile customization items and titles introduced

Bug fixes

  • Fixed awkwardness on certain buttons in Archive: ABSOLUTE
  • Fixed Rapture spawn cinematics in Campaign Chapter 49
  • Improved combat effects and animations for Marian: Queen and Snow White: Heavy Arms
  • Fixed an issue preventing certain accounts from progressing past the title screen
  • Improved Marian: Queen profile icon quality when configuring texture quality settings
