Mabinogi Mobile · August 19, 2026

8/20 (Thu) Update Notice

In the August 20 (Thu) regular maintenance update for Mabinogi Mobile, abnormal combat power increases and overlapping passive effects in the class and rune systems were fixed. Alongside skill balance adjustments for the Archer and Bard lines, various bug fixes for raids, Tower of Phantasms, and convenience features were applied.

Key changes

  • Fixed an issue where combat power abnormally increased or failed to apply when transcending armor rune engravings
  • Fixed an issue where passive effects from certain runes and skills were applied additionally and overlapped
  • Reduced knockback distance for the Archer skill 'Arrow Revolver'
  • Removed knockback and significantly reduced monster flight distance for the Bard skill 'Buzz Tale'
  • Improved skill structures for Bard's 'Spotlight' and 'Ad-Lib'
  • Changed pet food storage attribute to 'Available in Shared Storage'

Buffs

  • Added a feature to Spotlight extending the duration of 'Blessing of the Night' by 5 seconds
  • Added a feature ensuring Spotlight triggers guaranteed upon activating 'Encore'
  • Increased the maximum stack count of Ad-Lib to 2

Nerfs

  • Reduced Arrow Revolver's enemy knockback distance
  • Changed Buzz Tale so it no longer knocks back enemies
  • Fixed an issue where final damage boost effects overlapped when utilizing Encore and Finale in 4 tempos

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where 'Invitation to a Dream' was not used in White Succubus Raid Very Hard difficulty
  • Fixed an issue where 'Stone Breaker of Oblivion' disappeared on Tower of Phantasms floor 23-1
  • Fixed an issue where the 'Enchant Amplification Icon' was not displayed on enchanted equipment
  • Fixed an issue where an error popup appeared when entering already-used coupon numbers on the coupon redemption screen
  • Fixed an issue where monsters disappeared when a pet with a shield applied became incapacitated
