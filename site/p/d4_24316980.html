Diablo IV Patch 3.3.0 Notes

Diablo IV update 3.3.0 is now live, featuring Season of the Horadric Stones events and boss bug fixes. Horadric item drop rates have been increased and their stats overhauled, alongside major balance adjustments to class skills and Paragon boards for classes including Barbarian and Druid.

Key changes

  • Fixed enemy spawn issues during Helltide Tempest summoner and portal invasion events
  • Increased Horadric item drop rates and added Horadric Cache recipes
  • Adjusted core skills and aspects balance for Barbarian such as Frenzy and Double Swing
  • Split Druid skill tags and overhauled systems related to Spirit and Overpower
  • Buffed and overhauled the vast majority of Paragon boards and glyphs
  • Adjusted Unique item effects and performance for Druid and Barbarian

Buffs

  • Increased Horadric item drop rates
  • Increased Barbarian's Aspect of Limitless Rage damage bonus to 0.5-0.75%
  • Increased Barbarian's Aspect of Volatile Fissure damage to 80-120%
  • Increased Druid's Boulder base damage to 120%
  • Increased Druid's Pulverize shockwave variant damage to 300%

Nerfs

  • Reduced 5-piece bonus damage of Wrath of Sescheron to 500%
  • Reduced 2-piece bonus damage of Wrath of Sescheron to 80%
  • Reduced Charge Juggernaut variant burn damage to 126%
  • Reduced Shred variant effect radius to 100%

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where the Reaper boss would spawn one more time after the player died
  • Fixed an issue where the Reaper boss could be defeated with ranged attacks while on the upper level
  • Fixed an issue where legacy Mother's Cultists appeared in seasonal activities instead of Ulo Sar Cultists
  • Fixed an issue where Horadric stats of Song of the Eternal Mountain did not increase petrification duration
  • Fixed an issue where Ring of Red Fervor did not function fully when combined with resource cost reduction effects
