November 19, 2026 Patch Notes

Marvel Rivals is releasing new content and a 2nd-anniversary event on November 19, 2026, without downtime. This update includes new store items, Pop & Win events, Twitch Drops, and various hero bug fixes.

Key changes

  • Applied November 19 patch with zero server downtime
  • Opened Marvel Rivals 2nd Anniversary 'Rivals' Day 2026' and Times Square Galactic Center
  • Weekly attendance check event to earn up to 1,900 Units
  • Introduced new 'Pop & Win' option to choose whether to include emojis
  • Marvel Rivals IGNITE Grand Finals Twitch Drops underway
  • Improved hero-related features and fixed bugs for Nightcrawler, Black Cat, and Mantis

Bug fixes

  • Black Cat: Fixed an issue where Fortune was not granted upon hitting with the Turn of Fortune ability
  • Mantis: Fixed an issue where Life Energy Blast granted Life Orbs when hitting barriers and summons
  • Fixed an issue where the opening sound effect was missing from the Battle Pass season settlement screen
  • Resolved an issue where the competitive score interface did not update in real-time in Times Square
  • Fixed clipping issues with the diamond form sleeves and shoe soles of Emma Frost's 'Seat of Winter' costume
