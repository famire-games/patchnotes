Genshin Impact · May 20, 2026

Version "The Seventh Moon" Update

The Genshin Impact "The Seventh Moon" version update introduces new characters Nicole, Roane, and Prune, along with new Archon Quests and equipment. Various system optimizations and bug fixes have been implemented, and Primogems will be distributed as update compensation.

Key changes

  • Released new 5-star characters Nicole (Pyro, Catalyst), Roane (Cryo, Polearm), and 4-star character Prune (Anemo, Catalyst)
  • Added new 5-star weapons "Catalyst: Seven-Light Revelation", "Polearm: Calamity" and new Artifacts "Heaven's Grace", "Shattered Dreams in the Shadows"
  • Permanently unlocked Archon Quest Chapter IX and Chapter X of the Song of the Welkin Moon
  • Added new formidable enemies in the Lokapala Jungle area and unlocked the Blessing of the Welkin Moon Domain "Thorn Crown of the Mountain Wind"
  • Opened new rounds of Imaginarium Theater on June 1st and July 1st
  • Significantly expanded Sea of Stars World costumes, lobby themes, and custom features

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where the Elemental Skill was locked to a special skill when progressing through Jean's Story Quest with the Traveler
  • Fixed an issue where two chalkboards would appear or terrain clipping would occur in Speranza during Linnea's Story Quest
  • Fixed an issue where some field objects remained after completing the World Quest "O Life, Everlasting"
  • Fixed abnormal behaviors and actions of the enemies "Seasoned Frost Wolf" and "Fatui Skirmisher"
  • Fixed modeling errors when freezing Linnea on specific device environments and the inability to use normal attacks after reconnecting
