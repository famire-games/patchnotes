Overwatch November 17 Hotfix Patch Notes

A hotfix update has been deployed to Overwatch. Grouping restrictions for 6v6 Open Queue have been adjusted, and replay codes from the November 4 patch remain valid.

Key changes

  • Group wide-match threshold changes for 6v6 Open Queue

Nerfs

  • Max group width for groups containing Champion rank players reduced from 4 to 3 in 6v6 Open Queue
