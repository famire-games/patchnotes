Bungie Community Update - Destiny Content Unvaulting Plan

Bungie announced an unvaulting initiative to restore previously vaulted content to Destiny 2, aiming to rebuild trust and make the game whole. The first pass will bring back campaigns, strikes, raids from Red War, Forsaken, Curse of Osiris, and Warmind, alongside the Menagerie with reprised rewards for all players. Full production for this porting process is expected to take around 12 months following the current pre-production phase.

Key changes

  • Restoring Red War, Forsaken, Curse of Osiris, and Warmind campaigns
  • Bringing back associated Strikes, Raids, and Raid Lairs
  • Returning the Menagerie activity with reprised rewards
  • Full production for porting estimated to take around 12 months
