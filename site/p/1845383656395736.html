PSO2 New Genesis 10/6 Maintenance and Update Notice

UTC 2026-10-06 15:00 ~ 08:00

Server maintenance is scheduled from October 6 to October 7, bringing balance adjustments to Bouncer and Jet Boots, system and UI enhancements, new drop items, and multiple bug fixes. Players will be unable to log in during the maintenance window.

Key changes

  • Bouncer and Jet Boots class skill and photon art balance adjustments
  • Performance buffs for Special Equipment including Mobile Cannon ECS
  • Added Auto-Join Party feature to Alter Realm Exploration quests
  • Default additions to Character Creation and Salon option lists
  • Allowed Twaalv II, Legendios II, and Leyend II series weapons to be sold in Personal Shops

Buffs

  • Increased potency and reduced cooldowns for various Bouncer skills
  • Increased potency for multiple Jet Boots Photon Arts
  • Enhanced Mobile Cannon ECS PP recovery and Bullet Boost gauge performance

Bug fixes

  • Fixed issues preventing entry in certain areas of Major Target Suppression missions
  • Fixed potential effect duration and additional effect application bugs
  • Fixed graphic display errors for specific basewear and accessories
  • Fixed UI and quick menu toggle display bugs
