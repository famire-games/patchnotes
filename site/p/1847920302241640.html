Version 0.8.117 Update

Version 0.8.117 of Manor Lords has been promoted to the default branch. This update introduces new development perks, a fishmonger, a dunghill building, extensive farming and livestock overhauls, military adjustments, and numerous bug fixes.

Key changes

  • Added “Rural Foundations” Perk Category with six new perks, unlockable at Settlement Level 3
  • Added new Artisan: Fishmonger, producing Smoked Fish from Fish and Salt
  • Added new Building: Dunghill to collect Dung and produce Fertilizer
  • Official split of crops into Winter and Spring with mandatory Crop Rotation
  • Added various new development perks and building upgrades

Buffs

  • Warbows do 23% more damage
  • Rye’s fertility bonus increased from x1.5 to x1.75 and yield increased 7%
  • Livestock breeding chance for Cows and Sheep increased to 40%
  • Increased fishing spots number to 15 per Resource Node

Nerfs

  • Crossbows do 16% less damage
  • Decreased the output from the Deep Mine
  • Fertility boost gained from having Rich Fertility in a Region lowered by 30%
  • Crops grow 2.4% slower

Bug fixes

  • Fixed picking wrong Origin Perk when having rich Iron and rich Small game
  • Fixed exporting goods imported to fill a need
  • Fixed constantly firing and hiring workers at the Hunting Camp
  • Fixed not being able to build a Stonemason’s Station in a region without Rich Stone
  • Fixed constructing too much living space due to counting orchards as potential expansion plots
