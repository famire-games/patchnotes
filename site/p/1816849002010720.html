Baldur's Gate 3 · November 20, 2025

Hotfix #35 Released

This hotfix focuses on improving game performance and resolving crashes on consoles and the Steam Deck. Notably, the default upscaling method for the Steam Deck native build has been changed from FSR 1 to FSR 2.2 to enhance sharpness.

Key changes

  • Fixed major crashes and freezes on consoles and Steam Deck
  • Changed Steam Deck native default upscaling to FSR 2.2
  • Improved certain loading times and NVIDIA GPU VRAM usage on Steam Deck
  • Fixed external controller malfunction bug on Steam Deck

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a freeze that occurred when pressing L2 on PS5
  • Fixed Xbox crashes occurring when ending multiplayer sessions or suspending during loading
  • Fixed crashes on Steam Deck and Mac that occurred when saving the game after crafting
  • Fixed potential crashes for hosts and clients during crossplay
  • Fixed a startup crash on Steam Deck when the EN locale is missing
