Sea of Thieves Season 21 Update

Sea of Thieves Season 21 launches on September 17, bringing various live events, Custom Seas feature improvements, and a new Season Pass with rewards. A Talk Like a Pirate Day event and Free Play Weekend will also take place.

Key changes

  • Season 21 launches on September 17 with new free and premium Season Passes
  • Introduced a 'Layouts' feature in Custom Seas to save and load game states
  • Talk Like a Pirate Day event (Sept 17-22) and the new 'Flag of Negotiation'
  • Free Play Weekend across all platforms from September 17 to 21
  • Outpost shops stocked with new cosmetic items including costumes, weapons, and ship sets
  • Live events including Fight Night and Weekly Rewards, plus upcoming Community Weekends
