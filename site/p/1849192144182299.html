THE FINALS · November 12, 2026

Update 12.3.0 Patch Notes

Update 12.3.0 for The Finals includes the The Grand Major 2026 (TGM26) event and pass, esports bundles, overall balance adjustments such as the burning mechanic, Dome Shield, and Healing Emitter, along with various bug fixes.

Key changes

  • Released TGM26 Event Pass and esports bundles for 16 teams
  • Return of the popular LTM mode 'Head Hunter'
  • Adjusted performance for Dome Shield and healing items (Healing Emitter, H+ Infuser)
  • Overhauled the burning status effect mechanic and improved reliability
  • Increased cooldown and reduced glitch duration for Shockwave-specialized abilities
  • Slightly increased Translocator deployment time and fixed aim assist bugs

Buffs

  • Increased burning status maximum damage from 32 to 45 and duration by 1 tick
  • Increased H+ Infuser ammo capacity from 20 to 30

Nerfs

  • Reduced Dome Shield health from 600 to 400
  • Reduced Healing Emitter maximum healing per second from 20 to 15
  • Reduced H+ Infuser health restored per shot from 10 to 7
  • Increased Translocator deployment time from 0s to 0.3s
  • Reduced Shockwave building connection damage from 3,000 to 1,000, increased cooldown from 12s to 15s, and reduced Glitch effect duration from 2s to 1s
  • Reduced Flamestower impact damage from 30 to 26 and reduced heat generation per tick

Bug fixes

  • Improved animation blending during quick melee cancels
  • Fixed an issue where burning oil barrels would detonate instantly upon impact when thrown
  • Fixed an issue where allies hit by the H+ Infuser would shout 'Apres Fire!'
  • Fixed an issue where aim assist would target disguised Mimic Devices
  • Fixed an issue where Jump Pads failed to launch contestants holding onto ladders
