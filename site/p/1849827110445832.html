Phasmophobia Resonance Update

Performance and stability have been improved with the introduction of the Unity 6 engine, alongside an overhauled lighting system and improved voice recognition. New equipment such as the 'Dowsing Rod', an improved map voting system, and colorblind modes have been added.

Key changes

  • Completely overhauled performance, stability, and lighting systems via the Unity 6 engine
  • Added the 'Dowsing Rod', a new item for finding bones on larger maps
  • Overhauled map voting system allowing players to build custom random pools of desired maps
  • Overhauled voice recognition and added a list of interactive phrases to the journal
  • Added single/repeating sound icons for sound sensors on the truck map
  • Added various colorblind modes and XBOX Play Anywhere support

Buffs

  • Yurei door opening and closing speed slightly increased compared to regular ghosts
  • Poltergeists can now perform general interactions in addition to throwing objects

Nerfs

  • Goryo will no longer change rooms even if a 'Genie' wish is made

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where opening the journal while sprinting prevented stamina recovery
  • Fixed an issue allowing photos to be taken through the gaps of closed doors
  • Fixed an issue where ghosts could start hunts inside hiding spots
  • Fixed a softlock caused by tarot card usage overlapping with special buttons
  • Fixed an issue where the same equipment could be sold twice
